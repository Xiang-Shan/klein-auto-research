The experiment journey

Phase 0: two floors, and only one of them is a bar

Before any candidate ran, the study measured how much its own metric moves when nothing improves. It measured this twice, two different ways, and the two answers disagreed by everything.

Refitting the same anchor on the same rows under five different fit seeds moved the score not at all: standard deviation 0, range 0. A convex solver on a fixed partition is deterministic. This is the trap the whole discipline exists to avoid. A study that had pasted the cheap number into its contract would have carried a keep bar of zero and would have kept every candidate that moved the last decimal. Klein records the two under different keys — fit_noise for provenance, noise_floor for the bar — so the cheap one can never be mistaken for the expensive one.

The ladder

Four candidates, each one falsifiable idea, each committed before it ran. Every run printed its own distance to the ceiling and its paired lift over the rung it claimed to beat — the predecessor refitted on the same rows inside the same run, so the comparison lives in one printed block instead of being reconstructed afterwards.

The pair of panels is the lesson. On the left, in metric units, all five rungs look alike and the ceiling sits just above them — that is the honest picture, and it is why a reader staring at raw AUC cannot tell progress from noise. On the right, the same distances divided by the measured floor: the anchor is 10.4555 floors from the ceiling and the boosted rung is 1.7077. Same numbers, one division, completely different story.

The fourth rung, and the discard that paid for itself

Before spending the fourth candidate the study read its headroom: . Ajar, not open — so the detection-limit law neither refused the run nor demanded an acknowledgement. The study spent it anyway, on an over-capacity boosted tree with five times the trees, no shrinkage discipline and no early stopping.

It came back worse: delta_in_floors -1.7903 against its own reference, a discard. And it broke the probabilities long before it broke the ranking — log-loss went from 0.355165 to 1.024102, nearly triple, while AUC fell by only 0.013341. It also cost 18.407609 seconds against 0.647123.

That refuted a registered prediction. P4 was written as a no-effect prediction — “within noise” — and the honest reading of a refuted no-effect prediction is not “nothing happened after all” but “something happened, in the wrong direction”. The study wrote that down, dated, and stopped buying capacity.

The one sealed look

The sealed partition was rehearsed first — a dry run that executes the whole path on development data and spends nothing, because an earlier study lost its only seal to a crash that happened before any data was read. Then it was spent once. The selected configuration scored 0.880949 against that partition's own ceiling of 0.893469, a distance of 1.68 floors against 1.7077 on development.

The distance to the ceiling did not grow when the rows changed. That is what “the remaining gap is irreducible at this sample size” looks like when you can actually see the ceiling.

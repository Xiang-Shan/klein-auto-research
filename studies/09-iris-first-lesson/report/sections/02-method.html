Method

Two headroom numerators, and you must name which one you mean. Headroom is a ratio of how much room exists above the incumbent to how coarse the ruler is. This study measures it at two scales, and they do not share a numerator:

The ledger scale asks whether a keep is arithmetically possible for anybody against the one scalar bar the ledger enforces; its numerator is E0001's declared-split score, 0.026409. The candidate scale asks whether one named challenger's comparison can be won at its own resolution; its numerator is the anchor's marginal mean Brier across the twenty paired redraws, 0.04302915. Different quantities, different denominators, different questions — and quoting one as the other is a banned claim here, precisely because the two are close enough to swap by accident.

The floors are clearance bars, not tie bars. Twenty group-aware redraws of the non-sealed rows, all ten families scored on the identical rows in each redraw, so the challenger-minus-anchor difference is paired by construction rather than by adjustment. Per candidate:

The direction matters more than the formula. In study 07 a per-method floor was a bar that bought a draw — a candidate was rewarded for its own noisiness, and that construction is banned here by name. Here the floor is a bar the candidate must clear, so a wide band penalises the case of the very candidate that produced it. Same arithmetic, opposite incentive.

The ledger's single scalar takes the widest challenger floor and rounds it up to three decimals — hgbt's 0.07959988633051922 was binding:

Controls are excluded from that maximum by registration: a deliberately information-destroyed control may not set the ruler. Escalation is raise-only, and the floor is a location-invariant dispersion functional — no observed gain can tune it.

The arena. Ten seeded repeats of stratified group 4-fold over the non-sealed pool, with nested whole-group quota subsampling down to each nominal rung, the same rows served to every family. Stage A fits the anchor and the two controls only — 720 fold-evaluations — and commits the per-rung headroom table before any challenger arena number exists. Stage B then runs the seven-family roster for a further 1,680. A whole-group quota cannot always land on the number you asked for: at nominal rung 60 the realized training size is 60 rows in 23 of the 40 fold-evaluations and 59 in the other 17, while every lower rung landed exactly. The registered phrase is therefore always nominal rung 60, and "trained on 60 rows" without that qualifier is a banned sentence.

Open and closed rungs. Each rung carries its own bar,

where 0.005 is a registered constant, not an estimated noise quantity. A rung is OPEN when : the anchor's own mean error is at least as large as the bar, so a perfect oracle could clear it. Closure gets a descriptive label — ceiling-closed when the anchor is simply too near perfect, fog-closed when the lottery's own spread has outgrown the error.

The guard, named honestly. Bar-1 forms each cell's paired improvement over the anchor, summarises it per repeat, computes a t-statistic, and then applies Rademacher sign flips at the repeat level — all 1024 enumerated — simultaneously across a family of 42 cells, seven challengers by six rungs, fixed before the sweep ran. A cell clears at an adjusted score of at most 0.05, which on a grid of 1/1024 means at most 51/1024. This is a registered selection guard under a symmetry assumption the design does not supply — never family-wise error control, never a statement about irises at large. And because the family size sits inside the statistic, a guard score here is not comparable with one from study 08's 113-cell family; the contract bans that comparison outright.

Bar-2 needs all three at once: the guard cleared, the mean gain at least that candidate's own floor, and the rung open. Note which threshold governs what — governs the rung's openness, governs the candidate's bar. The two are never swapped.

The split law applies inside everything. Under this study's registered seed the twin pair landed in TRAIN, putting a multi-row group in the non-sealed pool. Study 08's argument that a row-level inner cross-validation was lawful — because no multi-row group sat in its pool — therefore does not port. Every internal cross-validation here receives precomputed group-aware split policies, the sealed coda's own base included. That amendment is load-bearing, not decorative.

The seal is a band check, and nothing else. The frozen analysis picks the branch mechanically and writes a manifest before the confirmation phase; the sealed registry entries read it, so no code is edited after selection. Branch A would spend two looks; Branch B spends one and leaves the challenger seal shut. A band carries no nominal coverage once a winner has been chosen against the same folds that produced it, and this study never describes its coda as estimating anything.

Discipline that ran before the first gate. A pre-gate staging smoke scored development and sealed Briers under a candidate split seed. Nobody read the logs; they were deleted; the seed was retired and the split re-registered before the consult acknowledgement, with a standing law added that subagent smokes run on synthetic frames only. Two further seed namespaces were re-registered on build-time disclosures — a simulation truth seed colliding with training-draw seeds, a sensitivity seed sitting inside the arena's own subsampling range. All three fixes landed before any gate was recorded, and the near-misses stay in the sweep docstrings so they remain auditable.

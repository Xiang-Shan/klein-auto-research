The interpretation, labeled as one

The anchor's standing on this data reads best as an inductive-bias match: four nearly sufficient, nearly Gaussian features — petal-only already carries most of the signal — an almost-linear boundary, small-to-medium n, and shared-covariance regularization. The same reading predicts its failure modes, and the simulation lane shows them: break equal covariance and QDA takes it; break linearity and the linear family lands behind a constant. 「口径：这是解读，不是登记过的分解量。」

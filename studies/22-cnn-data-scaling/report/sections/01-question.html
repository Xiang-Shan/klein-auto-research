The question

You have fitted a data-scaling exponent on small training sets. The decision is whether to quote it: how much is an exponent measured over 1.5 decades of training-set size worth for predicting the loss one more step out? This study makes that concrete on Fashion-MNIST with one fixed small CNN (23,946 parameters). It fits to the held-out cross-entropy at six training-set sizes, , freezes the law, and then trains once on the full fit pool of 59,992 images and scores the official test set once. The four research questions

  • RQ1. What is the exponent over N = 500 to 16,000? The registered prior was 0.1 to 0.6.
  • RQ2. Is a pure power law, with no irreducible term, adequate over that range? The registered bar was a log-log R² of at least 0.98.
  • RQ3. Does the law predict the full-pool loss within 20 percent?
  • RQ4. If it misses, is the measured loss above the law (a saturating curve makes a pure law optimistic)?

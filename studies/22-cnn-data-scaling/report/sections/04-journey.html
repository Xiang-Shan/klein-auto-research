The experiment journey

Two registered tracks, no frontier and no keeps. A registered track measures: every run is a pre-registered cell, recorded as measured or crash. There were no crashes, no discards and no sweeps. The ledger below is generated from the run manifests; the figures are drawn from the committed per-seed tables and manifests.

How the runs were executed, and what that does to the evidence

E0001 to E0006 (the six development cells) ran as detached jobs: the notary committed the candidate, a host executed it, and the receipt was collected and judged locally. Each manifest says mode: detached and observed_by_notary: false, so these are attested, not observed. Of the claims' evidence, only E0005 was re-executed under observation (rep:E0005@20261103T084144Z, difference 0). E0007 (the fit) and E0008 (the sealed cell) ran in the foreground under the notary, and the fit's replication rep:E0007@20261103T084735Z re-ran its arithmetic on the pinned cells, not the cells themselves. Further replication records for E0001 to E0004 and E0006 sit in the run directories, all with difference 0; they were filed after the claims were locked and no claim cites them.

Phase 1: the six development cells (primary track)

Ascending N was chosen for cheapness and to fail fast on the detached path. The first two cells already broke the script of a power law: N=1000 (E0002) came out worse than N=500 (E0001). The journal recorded it as measured and did not retry. The next section on findings shows that the inversion is present in all three paired seeds.

Phase 2: the fit (exponent track)

E0007 fits the pure law over the six pinned cells, and adjudicated P1, P2 and P5 by the notary's arithmetic, not by prose. The fitted exponent is inside the wide P1 range and below the sharper P5 range.

Phase 3: the sealed extrapolation (confirmation evidence, labelled separately)

The sealed cell E0008 is confirmation evidence, not part of the development series. Its dry run trained only 60 steps on development data and spent nothing; the real run trained the full 8437-step budget for three seeds, in the foreground, and read the test set once. The prediction came from the fit frozen at E0007, and the fit table was hash-checked before training began. Section 5 gives the verdicts.

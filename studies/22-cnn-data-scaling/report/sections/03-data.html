The data story

Fashion-MNIST: 28×28 greyscale images, 10 classes, four IDX files each pinned by sha256 in study.yaml. No pretrained weights are involved. The call is GO-WITH-CAUTIONS; no BLOCKER is open.

The gotcha: a tooling false positive on small silhouettes

The first audit used a 64-bit difference hash and flagged 56 percent of held-out images as near-duplicates of training images. None were byte-identical. On 28×28 silhouettes a 64-bit hash collides constantly, so this was a false positive of the tool, not a leak. The fix was an exact pixel-space criterion: nearest fit-pool image by pixel RMS, with copies at 4 grey levels or less removed. That removed 8 development images and 5 test images, and the re-run pixel audit passes with no held-out image within 4 grey levels of any fit-pool image. The dHash result is kept in the card verbatim and is not the criterion of record.

The cautions that carry into the findings

  • WARN: the partitions are not one draw. Development rows come from the train file and the sealed rows from t10k. The measured per-image standardized difference is +0.015, with no detectable first-order covariate shift, which is not proof of none, and the data card measured no loss gap. This is the origin of the cross-partition caveat on C16.
  • WARN: no group ids. Same-product, different-view twins cannot be audited.
  • NOTE: the chance-level check was deferred. It is not mechanizable in index mode and no later step took it up, which is why no negative control exists (section 5).

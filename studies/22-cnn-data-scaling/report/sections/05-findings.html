Findings & insights

The evidence block above is copied from the records. The verdicts below cite claim ids from the lock (22-cnn-data-scaling#Cn); the sentence of each claim is the one in claims.lock.

Verdicts on the four questions

  • RQ1, answered (C1). The pure law fitted to the six seed-mean development cells has exponent 0.1304 and prefactor 2.126. The prior's range was 0.1 to 0.6, so P1 is supported, but the exponent sits below the “most likely 0.2 to 0.4” band and below the sharper P5 range (refuted). The printed bootstrap spread, 0.1181 to 0.1411, is descriptive; a post-hoc per-seed-slope t-interval is 0.1033 to 0.1569.
  • RQ2, refuted (C2). Log-log R² is 0.7955 against the registered 0.98. The three smallest cells are flat at 0.8296, 0.9492 and 0.8408 where the law needs a decline.
  • RQ3, refuted (C3). Measured 0.3959 (seed sd 0.0090) against predicted 0.5066: a signed relative error of −0.2802, outside the 0.20 bar.
  • RQ4, refuted with the opposite sign (C4). The measured loss is below the prediction by 0.1107 nats, so the law was pessimistic. P4 and P6 are refuted.

Of the seven registered predictions, only P1 and P7 were supported. P7, the only sharp METHOD-gate prior that held, was about training dynamics rather than the curve: the full-pool cell's best checkpoint came at step 8050 of 8437 (C5). Erratum E1 notes that −0.2802 is the seed mean of per-seed errors (the registered definition); the signed error of the seed-mean loss is −0.2797. Verdict unchanged.

Surprises, and how much of each is actually known

The small end is flat, and N=1000 is worse than N=500. Local exponent over the three smallest cells is −0.0096 against 0.2127 over the three largest, so the curve steepens with N, contradicting the method card's expectation that it would flatten (C7). N=1000 sits above N=500 on nested, paired subsamples and the inversion is present in all three paired seeds, by +0.1195 on average (C8). Every cell's best checkpoint came at 0.954 or more of its step budget, and the three smallest cells shared one 400-step floor with 113.8, 56.9 and 28.4 epochs (C6).

Mechanism: a hypothesis, untested (C9). The status of C9 as locked: hypothesis, untested. It says the small end may be compute-limited, so that at a shared 400-step budget the loss would track steps more than data. No step-matched or step-extended small cell was run, so this stands untested, and rival readings fit the same evidence: the small-data region that Hestness et al. describe, and non-monotone learning curves of a fixed model. The N=1000 inversion itself is unresolved; checkpoint selection on 50- and 100-image inner sets is visibly optimistic at N=500, but that was not isolated.

Even the friendliest refit over-predicts. A post-hoc, unregistered refit over the three largest cells alone gives exponent 0.2127 and predicts 0.4254, still above the measured 0.3959 (C10). Whether the curve kept steepening past N=16000 is a cross-partition reading only, with a descriptive local exponent of 0.2674 that is confounded with the development-versus-test gap. The held-out over inner-validation loss ratios do not show the test set easier or harder (C11, re-scoped by erratum E2); this stays exploratory.

The printed interval is too narrow (C12). The bootstrap width is 0.0230 exponent units and the per-seed-slope t-interval has width 0.0536, which is 2.33 times wider. The card expected exactly this, and the interval prior held.

What this study did not do

  • The cross-partition caveat applies to C3, C4 and the sealed point. The six fit cells are scored on the development lane and the sealed cell on the official test set (C16). A same-partition control was not run.
  • No negative control was run — no shuffled-label cell and no check against the chance level ln 10. The record gives no reason. The one positive control, a synthetic harness recovering a planted exponent, covers the fit code only, not training or evaluation. The cell losses sit far below chance, which is a sanity bound, not a control.
  • Re-execution is thin. E0001 to E0006 are attested detached runs, and only E0005 was re-executed under observation by the evidence the claims cite. The replication of the fit repeats arithmetic on pinned cells.
  • The noise floor was never separately measured. Both tracks are registered, so no claim leans on a keep bar; the per-cell seed spread is the only noise measure, and the contract's minimum_delta is a resolution, not a measured floor.

Practitioner impact: what to change in a training recipe, and at what cost

No materiality block is registered, so nothing is priced. The change that the evidence motivates is to make the step budget grow with N across the whole fitted range, with no minimum that several cells share, and to check that no best checkpoint sits at the end of its budget (C13); motivation is the measured shape (C6 to C8) and not the untested mechanism. The cells were not matched in compute, so the exponent is a property of this protocol, not of the network alone. Measured on a 4-CPU x86_64 Linux VM with no GPU: the sealed 3-seed array took 1883.8 s of wall time, and wall time is informational only.

What to try next

  1. Step-extended small cells (tests C9). Re-run N=500, 1000 and 2000 with a step budget at least as large as the N=4000 cell's, 3 seeds each, same development lane. If the left end then declines and the six-cell exponent steepens, the compute-limited reading gains support; if it stays flat, the small-data-region reading does.
  2. A same-partition extrapolation control (tests C11 and C16). Train at a large N on the train lane and score the development lane, so the miss splits into its law and partition parts.
  3. Register a two-regime or floor law before data. Pre-register a smoothly broken power law or an fit on the same six cells and hold it against a fresh sealed point between N=16000 and the full pool; never refit on E0008. Run a shuffled-label negative control in the same study.
  4. Longer full-pool training (follows P7). The full-pool best checkpoint sits in the last 5 percent of its budget; measure how far the loss keeps falling with a larger budget to learn whether 0.3959 is data-limited or compute-limited.

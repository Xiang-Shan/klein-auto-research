The experiment journey

One track (primary, a frontier track) carries the whole study. Each development candidate is a three-seed array judged by the notary against the incumbent with the measured keep bar of 0.0438; the ledger below lists every run. Development evidence (E0001 to E0008, scored on the 1,709 development images) is kept apart from the single sealed confirmation run (E0009), which is excluded from the adaptive frontier by design and appears as a discard row.

Development frontier

Phase 0 measured the floor on the anchor (E0001) and set the bar. The pixel-linear baseline (E0002) came in 0.65 bars below the anchor, so the recipe CNN barely beat a stencil model. Plain batch normalization (E0003) collapsed to a macro-F1 of 0.3005: a bug in inference, not a verdict on the idea. Recomputing the running statistics on the training set (E0004) scored 0.8960, a gain of 4.04 bars over the anchor and the first keep.

What each candidate did, against its bar

After E0004, flip/rotation augmentation alone (E0005) moved the score by -0.0067, unresolved at this budget. A third conv block (E0006, +0.0326) and 4,000 steps (E0007, +0.0280) were positive on every seed pair but short of the bar by 0.0112 and 0.0158; the bar was not relaxed after the fact. The registered bundle of all four levers (E0008) scored 0.9461, +0.0501 over E0004 and 0.0062 above the bar, but its margins over the depth-only and schedule-only runs (+0.0175 and +0.0221) are inside the bar. The bundle's gain cannot be attributed to any one lever.

Seed spread: why the bar is not the whole story

The anchor's seed-block spread (sd 0.0472) is 3.94 times the item-bootstrap sd of 0.0120 that sets the bar, which falsified a prior from the method card. Once batch normalization was in, per-candidate seed sds fell to about 0.007 to 0.015, so the anchor's fit noise does not describe the incumbent.

Sealed confirmation (separate evidence)

After a mandatory dry run, the incumbent configuration was run once on the sealed official test split (E0009). The run refits the same recipe on train plus development, so it scores E0008's recipe, not E0008's weights. It scored a macro-F1 of 0.9465, +0.0004 against development, and the incumbent replicated with difference 0 (klein replicate). The final headroom of 1.23 means a further keep would need a macro-F1 of 0.9899, so the frontier is nearly closed under the anchor's bar. The bar was measured at the anchor, not at the incumbent (see section 7).

The data story

The source is sklearn.datasets.load_iris, restricted to the two species that overlap — setosa is trivially separable and is out of scope by design. The raw loader hands back exactly 100 flowers, 50 of each species, 4 measurement columns; that identity is asserted on the RAW load, not the prepared table, so a lawful data-gate row drop can never manufacture a false refutation of it (prediction P0). Every column is a genuine float64 centimetre measurement — sepal length, sepal width, petal length, petal width, ranges 4.9–7.9, 2.0–3.8, 3.0–6.9, 1.0–2.5 — and the target is a genuine integer 0/1, not a disguised string boolean. That is a rare, cleanly-typed result, and it was still checked in full by printing every column's sorted value set rather than trusted because the table “looked clean”: the mandatory value-pattern check exists precisely because dtype == "object" has silently hidden a categorical column before in this framework's own history.

The blocker an independent audit found and fixed

The mechanized four-row leakage audit (python -m kleinlib.leakage) failed its duplicate-row check on the unmodified 100-row table:

[FAIL] duplicate-rows: 1 duplicated row-content hash(es) straddle partitions (train/development=1, train/test=0, development/test=0)

The hard pair contains one exact-duplicate row: two virginica flowers, identical on all four measurements at (5.8, 2.7, 5.1, 1.9). Under the declared stratified split, seed 20260904, the two copies land on opposite sides of the train/development boundary — one in training, one in development. That is exactly the leakage mechanism the data-gate protocol names: a memorization-capable challenger (knn5 most directly) can score the development copy correctly for free off a byte-identical training twin, in precisely the paired comparisons this study's predictions are decided on.

The fix was made entirely inside prepare.py, with no edit to study.yaml: drop the duplicate's second copy, deterministically, before the table is written. A true duplicate carries zero information beyond its twin, so the fix costs nothing. prepare.py was re-run, the table re-profiled, the split re-drawn, and the audit re-run clean:

[OK]   split-reproduces: kind=stratified reproduces deterministically from study.yaml (train=49 development=25 test=25 rows)
[OK]   duplicate-rows: no duplicate row content straddles partitions
[OK]   group-overlap: N/A — split kind is not 'group'
12/12 checks passed: clean

The realized table is 99 rows, not the round 100 several contract documents describe in prose — a direct, deterministic consequence of dropping one duplicate. The split realizes 49 training / 25 development / 25 sealed flowers (development and test both land exactly on the intended 25, preserving the exchangeability the study's paired floors depend on), per-class counts train {versicolor 25, virginica 24}, development {versicolor 13, virginica 12}, test {versicolor 12, virginica 13}. Class balance shifted from an exact 50/50 to 50 versicolor / 49 virginica — a positive rate of 49.49% — because the dropped duplicate was virginica; negligible at this scale, and no registered prediction's arithmetic depends on the literal row count.

Two questions this study needed answered before trusting a single number

Does this copy of the table differ from Fisher's own? The UCI mirror of iris is documented to contain transcription errors relative to the original. An independent fetch and cell-by-cell diff against sklearn's bundled copy found exactly three cell differences across two rows — both setosa. Zero differences anywhere in the 100 versicolor/virginica rows this study actually models: the hard pair is byte-identical between the two sources.

Does the negative control fire correctly? A constant predictor and a label-shuffled predictor both score at chance on all three tracks (constant-chance val_auc=0.5000; shuffled-chance between 0.4391 and 0.5192), inside the tool's default margin. A pipeline that could not score chance at chance could not be trusted with a perfect score either.

Go / no-go

GO. One blocker was found, by a mechanized and unconditional check, and fixed with a one-row, zero-information deletion that touched no line of study.yaml. Every remaining item is a WARN or NOTE: the small-n design is already the subject of this study's own bootstrap and headroom machinery; the realized 49-row training partition is a documented, arithmetically inert deviation from the contract's round-number prose; the UCI discrepancy touches nothing in the rows this study models. This card's ruling was reached without opening a single file from the earlier study that examined the same species pair.

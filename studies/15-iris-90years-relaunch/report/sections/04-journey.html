Phase 1 — anchor and floor: know the instrument before comparing anything

E0001 is the identity anchor and the incumbent's level, in one cell. On the raw loader: 100 rows, 50 of each species, 4 features, and the three partitions sum back to the prepared total — P0 supported on all five clauses. Fisher's lda_all4, fit on the 49 training flowers and scored on the 25 development flowers: val_accuracy=0.96, val_errors=1, primary_metric(val_auc)=1.000000. P1 () and P2 ( errors) both supported, comfortably. The 2000-replicate bootstrap interval for that AUC came back ci_low=1.000000, ci_high=1.000000, ci_width=0.0 — and P3, which predicted an interval wider than 0.05, was refuted. The reason is arithmetic, not a bug: hand-inspection of the score dump showed all 13 development versicolor flowers scoring below 0.02 and all 12 virginica above 0.45, a clean rank gap. Resample 25 already-cleanly-separated rows with replacement and every resample is cleanly separated too — the percentile bootstrap has no variability left to report. That is the first sign of the pegging this whole study turns on.

Four registered Phase 0 sweeps then measured every floor this study would use, before any challenger ran:

  • sweep:fit_noise_svm_rbf / sweep:fit_noise_hgbt (seed-sweep, five seeds, same 49/25 split): both recipes reach AUC 1.0 on every seed — provenance about the fit, never a bar.
  • sweep:floor_modern (paired-bootstrap, 1000 replicates, pair (lda_all4, hgbt), fixed in study.yaml before the measurement): the AUC difference is exactly 0 on every one of the 1000 resamples — std 0, range 0.
  • sweep:floor_fisher (split-lottery, five redraws strictly inside the 74 non-sealed rows): lda_all4's own development AUC is 1.0 on every redraw — std 0, range 0.
  • sweep:floor_ablation (paired-bootstrap, 1000 replicates, pair (lda_all4, lda_sepal)): a real, resolvable spread — mean -0.190092, std 0.0952175, range 0.5625, so .

Two of three tracks measured a keep bar of exactly 0. That is a measured zero, pasted into study.yaml and re-recorded at the consult gate, not an unmeasured placeholder. Klein's headroom law returns no number at all, h = None, whenever minimum_delta <= 0 — not “infinite headroom”, undefined headroom — which meant seven of this study's sixteen predictions (P4–P8, P10, P11) could never print the floor-normalized key their own registered rule needed. Each one's own inconclusive_if clause, written before Phase 0 measured anything, already named this exact condition. What it did not anticipate was that the floor would be measured at zero rather than simply left unmeasured. This was flagged as an explicit phase-boundary decision rather than resolved quietly: the orchestrator relayed the user's decision to run the parade anyway, with the structural inconclusiveness accepted as the honest, pre-registered outcome — not re-litigated, contract unedited.

Phase 2 — the parade: does ninety years of research beat him?

E0002 seeds the modern frontier with Fisher's own lda_all4, refit on the identical split: primary_metric=1.000000, matching E0001 to the printed digit — the two tracks are reading the same rows. Then four challengers, each refitting lda_all4 as its reference inside the same run, on the same development rows, so every comparison is paired by construction:

  • E0003 — L2 logistic regression: val_auc=1.000000, delta_vs_reference=0.0. An exact tie.
  • E0004 — 5-nearest-neighbours: val_auc=0.990385, delta_vs_reference=-0.009615. The parade's one genuine loss, and it is exactly the mechanism P6 predicted: with only 25 development rows, knn5 can only ever print six distinct scores (proba_unique_values=6), and every tie costs ranking resolution.
  • E0005 — RBF-kernel SVM: val_auc=1.000000, delta_vs_reference=0.0. Another exact tie, reproducing the Phase-0 seed-sweep's own finding.
  • E0006 — the boosted tree: printed val_auc=1.000000, delta_vs_reference=-0.0; the raw stored value is 0.9999999999999999, a floating-point hair below the reference, indistinguishable from a tie at print precision. val_accuracy=1.0, val_errors=0 — the one number in this cell that is not tied.

The disposition quirk: how a tie becomes a keep

With minimum_delta measured at 0, the frontier rule degenerates to — a condition an exact tie satisfies. Four of the five modern-track development cells (E0002, E0003, E0005, E0006) filed as keep; only E0004 discarded. Read the ledger above by status alone and it looks like three of four post-1936 methods improved on Fisher. None of them resolvably did: delta_vs_reference is 0.0 or -0.0 on every single keep. This is the exact mechanism the study's own stop-rule check confirms did not fire — only one discard occurred in the whole parade, never four consecutive, so klein stop ack was never needed — but it is also the reason prediction P9 (“zero of the four families earns a keep”) reads refuted on the letter of its own arithmetic (the count is four, not zero) while its substance — no post-1936 method separated the species measurably better — is supported.

Phase 3 — the ablation map: which measurements carry it

The ablation track's floor is not degenerate — 0.28125, measured from real resampling spread — so every prediction here resolves by ordinary registered arithmetic, the first phase where nothing reads inconclusive.

  • E0007 — petal-only LDA against the all-four reference, refit paired in the same cell: val_auc=1.000000, delta_vs_reference=0.0, delta_in_floors=0.0. P12 supported, the tightest possible margin: petal length and petal width alone land exactly on the all-four score.
  • E0008 — sepal-only LDA, same construction: val_auc=0.814103, delta_vs_reference=-0.185897, delta_in_floors=-0.661. P13 refuted — but read that refutation carefully. An 18.6-point AUC loss is real, large and correctly directed; it simply does not clear the pre-registered bar of a full floor below, because the ablation floor itself (0.28125) was measured from this exact contrast's own paired-bootstrap spread — nearly a third of the whole 0-to-1 AUC scale. A registered floor-normalized rule and a raw effect size can tell different stories on the same run.
  • E0009 repeats E0007's comparison with the boosted tree in place of LDA — the parade's incumbent, chosen because the parade itself left no substantive ranking to pick a “best” family from (every keep tied), so hgbt is both the track's current incumbent and the family least like LDA, the hardest available test of the claim: delta_in_floors=0.0. P14 supported — the petal verdict is a property of the flowers, not an artifact of the discriminant.

The bottom panel of that figure is the whole study in one picture: on fisher and modern the shaded “cannot resolve” band has zero width, so even hgbt's later sealed loss sits just outside a band too thin to see. On ablation the band is wide enough that sepal-only's very real shortfall still falls inside it.

Phase 4 — confirmation: three sealed numbers, spent once each

Every sealed cell was rehearsed first with klein run-one --final-test --dry-run — three rehearsals, each printing sealed_dryrun: 1 on development data and spending nothing.

E0010, the fisher track's sealed access: lda_all4 refit on the 74 train-plus-development rows, scored on the 25 sealed flowers nobody had looked at: val_auc=1.000000 again — the same peg, reproduced on a different 25-row block, so it is a property of this species pair under these four measurements, not one lucky development draw. But val_errors rose from 1 to 2 at the ordinary threshold of 0.5, and val_accuracy fell from 0.96 to 0.92: the rank statistic transferred exactly, the raw error count did not.

E0011, the modern track's sealed access: the selected challenger, hgbt, refit on the 74 rows and scored against lda_all4 refit on the same sealed rows in the same run: val_auc=0.987179 against a reference of 1.0 — delta_vs_reference=-0.012821, exactly two of the block's 156 ordered pairs. val_accuracy=0.88, three flowers misclassified against Fisher's two on the identical rows. Predictions P10 and P11 both read inconclusive — the floor-normalized keys they need are still unprintable at minimum_delta=0 — but this is the single most informative raw number the whole modern track produced: every development-side comparison in the parade printed a tie, and on rows neither model had ever seen, the ninety-years-later challenger lost.

E0012, the ablation track's sealed access: all three feature sets refit on the 74 rows, scored on the same 25 sealed flowers in the same run. Petal-only: val_auc=0.987179, delta_in_floors=-0.0456 — comfortably inside the floor, confirming P12's development-side verdict on rows nobody had adaptively touched. Sepal-only: sepal_delta_in_floors=-0.8547 — a real, large, correctly-directed shortfall, wider than on development, that again falls just short of the registered floor bar. P15 refuted on that second clause, for the identical structural reason E0008 gave.

Finally, klein replicate re-executed the development run behind every confirmed claim in a detached worktree: E0001, E0006, E0007 and E0008 all reproduced exactly, difference 0.0. (A sealed run cannot itself be replicated — re-running it would be a second look at the sealed partition — so the replicate leg is filed against the development run each sealed cell's config and comparison rest on. E0001's first replication attempt timed out at the default budget with no output at all, a cold-worktree startup cost, not a failed reproduction; the retry, given a longer budget, reproduced exactly, and both attempts stayed on the record.)

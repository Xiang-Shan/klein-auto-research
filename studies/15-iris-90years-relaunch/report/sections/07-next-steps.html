Next steps + references

What to try next, in priority order

  1. Re-run the identical parade with a primary metric that cannot peg. This is the direct repair of the pegging finding. Log-loss or Brier score on the same 49/25/25 partition is unbounded below only by perfection, and both were already observed to vary widely across these same five recipes while ROC-AUC sat frozen at 1.000000: development log-loss ranges from 0.012827 to 0.172887, Brier from 0.000945 to 0.056. A paired-bootstrap floor on (lda_all4, hgbt) under log-loss would almost certainly be non-degenerate, restoring delta_in_floors and every prediction rule built on it. Register both a floors rule and a raw-delta twin, so a future degenerate floor cannot silence a prediction outright.
  2. Measure how much of the pegging is the 25-row block and how much is the species pair. A registered estimate track with a split-lottery over many more redraws than this study's five, inside the non-sealed rows, reporting the distribution of lda_all4's development AUC and of its threshold-0.5 error count — the error count is the one statistic this study already showed moving (one flower on development, two on the sealed block) where the AUC did not.
  3. Retest the ablation with a floor recipe that is not measured on the contrast under test. This study's ablation floor was set by the same paired comparison it then had to adjudicate, making the one-floor bar unreachable for a genuinely large effect. Measure the floor instead on a null contrast — the same feature set against itself across bootstrap draws — so the bar reflects noise rather than the effect's own tail, then re-adjudicate the sepal clauses against it.
  4. Add the one recipe this contract deliberately excluded. A tabular foundation model pre-trained for exactly this row-count regime is the honest 2025-era frontier answer and was named in the method card specifically as outside this study's parade, because adding a sixth recipe after the gate would be a contract change. A fresh study can register it at consult. Under a non-pegging metric, it is the one candidate with a plausible route to a resolvable win — and if it too ties Fisher's 1936 discriminant on a metric with headroom, that is a far stronger statement than this study could make.

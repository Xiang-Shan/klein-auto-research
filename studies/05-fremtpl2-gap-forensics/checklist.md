# Checklist — dataset characteristics → method choice

> The transferable artifact of study **05-fremtpl2-gap-forensics**. Read THIS to decide;
> read [`findings.md`](findings.md) to audit the decision. Every row cites the claim that
> earned it — fully qualified form `05-fremtpl2-gap-forensics#C<n>`, cited from
> `knowledge/` with a typed verb (`supports …#C6` / `refutes …#C7`).
>
> Scope honesty: these rows were earned on ONE dataset (freMTPL2 claim frequency,
> 678,013 rows, 9 features, exposure-weighted Poisson, single seed-42 split). They are
> *starting priors with measured magnitudes*, not laws. Each row tells you the cheap
> measurement to run on your own data before trusting it.

Never price it against a differently-encoded incumbent. | BonusMalus is non-decreasing in frequency over **100% of train exposure** marginally, so `+1` is the right sign. Enforcing it still cost **+0.002112 = 3.7× the gbdt floor** vs a same-encoding control, and +0.002766 = 4.8× floor vs the OHE incumbent (E0009). The constrained model captures **72.8%** of the anchor gap — filing the shape gives up **27% of the boosted edge**. Mechanism: sklearn enforces monotonicity *pointwise in every conditioning context*, and the model wants the BM slope to vary by vehicle age. | `#C7`, `#C13` |
| 7 | **The noise floor that governs the comparison you are about to publish** — and your split's hygiene | Measure THREE things before quoting any gap: (a) fit-seed std over k≥5 seeds per model; (b) a **paired** bootstrap SE of the DIFFERENCE using common random numbers (B≈1000); (c) the share of eval rows whose FEATURE profile has a twin in train. | **Bootstrap the difference, not the two levels** — the marginal fold SE overstates comparison noise several-fold. Then **report the GAP, never the absolute levels**: contamination and fold-frequency drift hit every model equally, so the difference survives what the levels do not. Seal a partition per track and confirm the gap as a difference of two sealed numbers before you publish it. | Floors here differ by ≥4.6×: GLM fit-seed **exactly 0** (deterministic solver — a seed floor for a GLM is meaningless), GBDT fit-seed 0.000210, within-track paired SE 0.000270/0.000287, **cross-track paired SE 0.000963** (the only one that bands the gap). Hygiene: **26.54%** of dev rows and 26.37% of sealed-test rows have a feature-identical train twin, and fold frequency drift alone flipped every model's A/E from +2.2% (dev) to −0.4%/−1.0% (sealed) with no model change. The gap still replicated: sealed **0.009564 = 9.3× the sealed SE**, within **1.07× SE** of the development gap. | `#C1`, `#C9`, `#C11` |

## How to use this in 15 minutes

1. Count your rows → **row 1**. If you are under ~40k, stop here and build the GLM well.
2. Fit one default GBDT, distil it, read `R²_main` → **row 2**. This is the single number
   that tells you whether an interpretable rebuild can ever close the gap.
3. If the gap is non-additive and you must file an interpretable model, run the screen with
   a published multiplicity budget → **row 3**, and price your shape constraints → **row 6**.
4. Do not spend slots on encoder or library shootouts until rows 4 and 5 say your data is
   outside the regime where they are ties.
5. Before any of it becomes a number in a document, do **row 7**. It is the cheapest row and
   the only one that can invalidate all the others.

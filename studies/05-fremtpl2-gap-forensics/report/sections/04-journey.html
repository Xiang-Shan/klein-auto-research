The Journey

Twelve ledger rows across two tracks and four phases, every one still resolvable to the exact code that produced it — the loop commits each candidate before it runs, so discards and crashes stay as resolvable as keeps. Every phase finished far inside its budget — the entire study is well under a minute of compute.

Two tracks, two frontiers

Each track's development frontier is a step function of keeps; discards sit below or above it as retained evidence; the purple star is that track's single sealed final-test access, deliberately drawn off the frontier because sealed evidence is excluded from adaptive selection.

adaptive-1 — anchors, then metrology

Nothing may be compared before the ruler is calibrated. This phase did two jobs: prove the pipeline reproduces study 04 exactly, and measure the floors.

Friction F1 — a burned slot, and it teaches something. E0001 printed the anchor-exact metric 0.454861 and was still dispositioned discard — "guardrail metric wall_seconds missing". Cause: evaluate_regression writes wall_seconds into aux_metrics.tsv but does not print it in the canonical block, and the runner's guardrail check reads the printed block. Study 04 had avoided this by passing wall_seconds explicitly via extra — a line that looked decorative and was load-bearing. The fix was E0002's one-line candidate diff; adaptive-1's max_experiments was raised 2→3 to un-burn the slot, and the reason was recorded in the contract description rather than quietly patched.

E0002 and E0003 then reproduced study 04's published anchors to 1e−9 through a different metric implementation (the v0.4.0 registry against study 04's hand-rolled deviance): glm_ohe 0.454861, hgbt_ohe 0.444689. Anchor gap +0.010172.

The phase's headline discovery was an aux column nobody would have logged without the method card. Risk R1 forced effective_trees into the aux ledger on every GBDT row, and E0003's HistGradientBoostingRegressor(max_iter=200) came back having fitted 67 trees — early_stopping="auto" switches on above 10,000 samples and holds out 10% of the train fold. The nominal capacity setting was a third of the truth, invisible in the config.

Then the floors, all as sanctioned sweeps with sidecar TSVs:

gbdt fit-seed sweep  (k=5)   ->  std 0.000210
paired CRN bootstrap B=1000  ->  glm-pair SE 0.000270   -> minimum_delta 0.000539
                                 gbdt-pair SE 0.000287  -> minimum_delta 0.000573
                                 CROSS-track SE 0.000963

dev gap +0.010172  =  10.6x the cross-track paired SE

adaptive-2 — the forensics ladder

The phase opened with the slate ritual: six falsifiable candidates scored on novelty / testability / expected information, the table recorded before any of them ran.

Candidate 3 went first because its verdict decides what the rest of the study is about: if additive shaping closes a third of the gap, the translate-back story is "splines plus interactions"; if it closes a sixth, interactions are the only hope. It closed a sixth.

  • E0004 KEEP — column-scoped splines, 0.454861 → 0.453156. A real frontier improvement (3.2× the glm floor) that closes only 16.8% of the gap. RQ4's 30–45% prior refuted in magnitude on the very first candidate.
  • E0005 discard — LightGBM-Poisson, 0.444413, i.e. 0.48× floor better than HGBT. A tie, as predicted — but recorded with the capacity confound stated in the description (200 LGBM trees vs HGBT's effective 67).
  • E0006 discard — CatBoost, 0.446332, +2.9× floor worse. A real deficit, not a tie.

Then the loop stopped and ran the instruments (off-ledger — analysis, never evidence). The train-fold surrogate returned R²_main = 0.6647: only two thirds of the teacher's log-rate surface is main-effects-representable. The gap is substantially non-additive, and M4-a's "> 0.90" prior was falsified. The residual ranking:

rank 2   BonusMalus x logDensity    0.0985      PD cross-check strength 0.043
rank 3   VehAge x DrivAge           0.0789      PD cross-check strength 0.016
...

The segment decomposition (M5) came back DIFFUSE: the top-3 BonusMalus bins carry 87% of the gap on 89% of exposure — essentially proportional to exposure. No bad segment to carve out.

  • E0007 discard — scoped splines + the rank-1 product, 0.452926: 0.43× floor. Sub-floor.
  • E0008 discard — both top pairs, 0.452913: 0.45× floor. Still sub-floor.

Multiplicity ledger: 10 pairs screened, 0 adopted — and both rejections filed as ledger rows rather than deleted. That published zero is the phase's real product.

adaptive-3 — the post-forensics redirect

The forensics reframed the remaining questions, so the reserve slots went to mechanism rather than to more library shootouts.

  • E0009 discard — hgbt_monotone, 0.447455. Priced two ways, per method-card risk R2: against a same-encoding unconstrained control (study 04's native HGBT 0.445343, identical split and prep, tag v1.0.0) the constraint alone costs +0.002112 = 3.7× floor; against the OHE incumbent, constraint plus encoding switch costs +0.002766 = 4.8× floor. The constraint also cut effective capacity to 53 trees.
  • E0010 discard — LightGBM truncated to n_estimators=67, matching HGBT's effective capacity: 0.444431, Δ = 0.45× floor. The tie shrank under matching, so RQ2's tie is algorithmic, not an early-stopping artefact.

Two more off-ledger instruments fired here. M3-a checked the empirical shape before interpreting E0009's cost: exposure-weighted mean frequency is non-decreasing in BonusMalus over 100% of train exposure (the 8-bin qcut collapsed to 4 usable bins because 68.8% of exposure sits in BM 50–54). The +1 sign is not merely defensible — it is right. And the RQ6 data-volume sweep refit both anchor configs on nested train subsamples, scoring each on the unchanged dev fold: seven fits, ~14 s.

confirmation — the two sealed accesses

Friction F2 — the second burned slot, and a framework bug. E0012 was refused with "sealed final-test state is missing for track 'gbdt'". Cause: klein new scaffolds a single track and initial_state derives the final_holdout_access keys from the tracks at scaffold time; adding a second track by editing study.yaml — the only way to build a two-track study today — leaves that map stale, and load_state performs no reconciliation. Fixed by an atomic scripted edit adding the missing gbdt entry, byte-identical in shape to what initial_state generates, committed with a note. Both frictions are filed as framework-fix candidates in section 7.

Then, one access each, on the incumbent each track had already selected:

E0012 also reproduced study 04's sealed HGBT number bit-identically across a study boundary — determinism nothing currently guards.

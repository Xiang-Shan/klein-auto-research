What to try next, in priority order

  1. Tensor-product spline / GAM interactions on the glm track — the real test of RQ5. E0007/E0008 tested raw standardised products, the crudest possible interaction basis, and found 0.43–0.45× floor. The surrogate says the structure exists (R²_main = 0.6647) and names where it is (VehAge×BonusMalus). Fit a tensor-product spline basis, or a bivariate smooth, on that one pair inside the scoped-spline GLM. Highest-information follow-up: it decides whether "83% non-additive" means GLM-unrepresentable or merely not representable by products. Prediction to falsify: closes 2–5× the glm floor — enough to keep, far short of the residue.
  2. Categorical interaction probe — close the numeric-only blind spot, and spend the slot the isolation lever never got. Extend the screen to categorical×numeric and categorical×categorical structure (Region×DrivAge, VehBrand×VehAge) using the two-way PD instrument on the fitted HGBT rather than the numeric-only surrogate, and re-run the segment decomposition over Region and VehBrand levels instead of BonusMalus bins. In the same phase, run catboost_poisson with one_hot_max_size=32 so C3's CTR-versus-capacity attribution stops being open.
  3. A two-track CI anchor test. Both anchors reproduced to 1e−9 across a study boundary, and E0012 reproduced study 04's sealed number bit-identically. Promote the pair (0.454861 / 0.444689 on the frozen prep SHA-256 db82e802…1cf948) into a repo-level regression test, so that any future change to the study library, the metric registry, or the environment that moves either anchor fails CI loudly. Determinism this exact is an asset; nothing currently guards it.
  4. Framework fixes — both cost a real experiment slot in this study.
    • F1 — evaluate_regression writes wall_seconds to aux_metrics.tsv but does not PRINT it, and the runner's guardrail check reads the printed block; E0001 was dispositioned discard with an anchor-exact metric because of it. Fix: print wall_seconds in the canonical block, or have klein preflight fail when a declared guardrail metric is not among the keys the run will print.

  5. Severity / pure-premium extension. Frequency is one of two factors in the premium. The same two-track sealed-gap protocol on a Gamma severity track and a Tweedie pure-premium track would say whether the 83% non-additive residue is a frequency phenomenon or a property of this book — and whether the 20–41k crossover moves when the response gets noisier.

And the artifact to start from

The transferable product of this study is checklist.md — seven rows of measurable property of your data → method choice it implies, each citing the claim that earned it and each naming the cheap measurement to run before trusting it. Its 15-minute protocol:

  1. Count your rows. Under ~40k, stop and build the GLM well.
  2. Fit one default GBDT, distil it, read R²_main. This single number tells you whether an interpretable rebuild can ever close the gap.
  3. If the gap is non-additive and you must file an interpretable model, run the screen with a published multiplicity budget, and price your shape constraints.
  4. Do not spend slots on encoder or library shootouts until your cardinality and capacity numbers say you are outside the regime where they are ties.
  5. Before any of it becomes a number in a document, measure your floors. It is the cheapest step and the only one that can invalidate all the others.

Scope honesty: those rows were earned on ONE dataset — freMTPL2 claim frequency, 678,013 rows, 9 features, exposure-weighted Poisson, a single seed-42 split. They are starting priors with measured magnitudes, not laws.

Honest positioning. None of the five methods taught here is a frontier architecture; all are well-established. What this study contributes is not a new method but a protocol — deriving translate-back structure on train, adopting it on development under a reported multiplicity budget, and confirming the resulting gap as a difference of two sealed test numbers. Reference 10 does the forensics without the seal; study 04 did the seal on one track only. Doing both at once is the gap in the literature this study fills.

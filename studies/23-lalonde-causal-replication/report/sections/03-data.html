Three pinned files, three lanes

The data are Dehejia's own NBER files, each pinned by sha256 and checked at the DATA gate: the NSW experimental sample (185 treated and 260 control men), PSID-1 (2,490 survey men) and CPS-1 (15,992). The split is predefined: the lanes are the design, so nothing is partitioned by a seed. NSW is the training lane, PSID-1 the development lane, and CPS-1 the sealed test lane; the sealed lane's 1978 earnings were never examined during development. All three have exactly the published sample sizes, and the PSID-1 and CPS-1 earnings means agree with Dehejia-Wahba's Table 1, a file-identity check.

The value-pattern check and its gotchas

Every column was inspected by value, never by dtype. The indicators hold only 0 and 1; there are no sentinels, no string-coded values and no missing entries in any lane. Earnings are non-negative with exact zeros that mean "not working" rather than "missing", and zero mass is large in the experiment (about three in four trainee-sample men have zero 1974 earnings). Two further points matter downstream. First, treat is zero for every PSID-1 and CPS-1 row: the treated group is always the NSW 185, so a model must pool them with the lane's comparison men, and the lanes are not self-contained treatment-control datasets. Second, the leakage audit passed (8 of 8 index-table checks), after a harness defect in the dataframe mode, which could not reproduce a predefined split under a scalar task, was fixed in the framework rather than waived.

Why the study exists: the comparison groups are unlike the trainees

The ranked cautions are the object of study. Comparison men differ from trainees by more than 0.8 standard deviations on six of eight covariates before any adjustment: for PSID-1 the standardized mean differences include -1.84 in married, -1.77 in re75, -1.72 in re74 and 1.48 in black, and CPS-1 differs by 2.43 in black. The NSW treated-versus-control row, by contrast, is balanced as randomisation predicts. Earnings are heavy-tailed, so means are outlier-sensitive, and with only 185 treated men every estimate is noisy. Nothing is trimmed in prepare.py; common support is a specification choice made later, in the estimator.

The call

The data card's decision is GO, with cautions: the three lanes carry the exact published sizes, fixed provenance, no missingness, no sentinels and a split that reproduces from the contract alone. The hazards (imbalance, zero-heavy earnings, small treated n) are disclosed as warnings and travel into the method and experiments as things to measure.

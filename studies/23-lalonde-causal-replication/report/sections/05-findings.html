The evidence behind every verdict

Verdicts below cite claims by id. The block here is generated from the lock and the study state, so the counts, strengths, errata and the referee's verdict are records, not prose.

The naive contrast is far off, and the experiment row reproduces

The first finding is LaLonde's lesson in one row: the naive difference in means against PSID-1 is -15204.78, against an experimental 1794.34, the wrong sign and a miss larger than the benchmark itself; against the sealed CPS-1 lane it is -8497.52 (claim C2). The two panels below put every design on one axis, then show how well each published target reproduces.

The reproduction verdict is mixed but precise. Of the registered targets, 9 of 11 on the development lane and 3 of 5 on the sealed lane reproduce, and the four misses are exactly the comparison-group adjusted values and their standard errors, against targets transcribed from the NBER working paper alone (claim C30). The classical OLS standard-error formula matches on every target that can be checked, so the worry that the paper used a different formula did not materialise. The likeliest reason for the misses, an exploratory reading found at METHOD, is that the working paper's Table 3 column (2) appears to mix two Table 2 columns (claim C10). That reading rests on one transcription, and the confirmed content is only the measured equalities and misses (claim C26). A transcription error in those four values would read as a miss, and only a second source can rule that out.

Propensity estimators on PSID-1: one of three lands in the band

With the Dehejia-Wahba logit, only AIPW lands within the registered 1000 dollars of the benchmark, with gaps of +1244.09 for IPW, -1234.06 for 1-NN matching and +889.48 for AIPW (claim C23). IPW and matching miss by almost the same amount on opposite sides, and the three estimates span 560.28 to 3038.44, wider than the benchmark itself (claim C31). The bootstrap ranges all contain the benchmark, but they hold the experimental controls fixed and the matching one is no valid interval, so containment is not evidence that an estimator recovers it. On weights, what was measured is the largest single weight share (0.135 for IPW and AIPW, 0.146 for matching) and 56 distinct matched men, not an effective sample size, so this study says the weights are concentrated, not that an estimate rests on a few people (claim C7).

Carried to the sealed lane

The selected AIPW estimator, with the PSID-1 covariate list unchanged and its logit and outcome model re-fit on CPS-1, estimates an ATT of 1327.54 with a bootstrap range from -129.17 to 2835.62, a gap of -466.80 against the benchmark (claim C29). That is within the band, once, for an estimator chosen using the development answer key, and the range does not exclude zero. Its largest weight share fell to 0.0426. The strength of this claim means one sealed run plus an exact re-execution of a development cell of the same pipeline, never a replicated sealed result.

Surprises

  • Which sign the misses take. The method card expected every out-of-band PSID-1 gap to be negative. IPW's was positive, so that prior fell. Of the card's eight staked priors, 7 held (claim C9).
  • P5 could not have failed for the reason it was written. It asked whether IPW without 1974 earnings misses by more than 1000 dollars, but IPW with them already did, so the rule would have been supported even had 1974 earnings been irrelevant (claim C13). The cell also did more than drop one covariate: it removed three terms, added a 1975 flag and moved the support from 1146 to 1092 men, so the shift from 3038.44 to -15.33 belongs to that whole specification change (claim C22). Whether 1974 earnings alone is load-bearing is open.
  • Matching reproduced the paper's control count but not its estimate (56 men, but 560.28 where the paper prints a much higher figure). Unresolved; no mechanism is claimed.
  • The sealed gap is smaller than the development gap and of the other sign. Better CPS-1 overlap is only one candidate: the pre-weighting balance points both ways, and one sealed draw cannot separate any mechanism from luck (claim C28).

What a decision-maker should take from this

The estimand is the average effect of the programme on 1978 earnings for the men who took it. The experimental answer is 1794.34, and every propensity row rests on selection on observables plus overlap, which this study does not test. With only PSID-1 you could not have told which of three estimates was right, so report the set, not one number. With the larger CPS-1 pool, the selected estimator agrees with the benchmark once, which is consistency and not validation. Changing the score's specification moved the answer by more than the benchmark's size, so the result depends on the specification, and this study ran one specification per estimator. What would change the conclusions: a placebo outcome that does not come out near zero, a specification family in which the CPS-1 gap leaves the band, or an interval that also resamples the experimental controls. Two controls are worth naming honestly: the NSW experimental reproduction is the positive control, and no placebo (negative) control was run. The four PSID-1 att cells are a family of four comparisons with no family-wise adjustment, so they stay exploratory; the sealed cells and the deterministic reproduction counts are not families.

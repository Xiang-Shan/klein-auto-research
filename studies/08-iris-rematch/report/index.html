The Rematch: 21 Challengers, a Data Ladder, and a Door That Stood Ajar

Large-budget rematch: under a selection-honest registered protocol, can any of 21 modern challenger families (incl. the 2025 TabPFN v2 foundation model) beat the 1936 LDA anchor on the versicolor-virginica hard pair - at full n or anywhere down the data ladder

The Question

Study 07 asked whether ninety years of method history could beat Fisher's 1936 rule on his own hard pair, and answered: no keep was earned. That answer had a hole in it, and the hole was arithmetic. Brier scores cannot go below zero, so the most any challenger could have gained over the 1936 anchor was the anchor's own score — and on that study's split-lottery mean, the anchor's score was 0.57× the floor that decides whether a difference is worth acting on. A keep-sized win was not merely unobserved. It was unavailable.

So this study re-asks the question with the arithmetic in the open, a roster three times larger, and a data ladder underneath it:

  1. The ledger bar. On a freshly drawn declared split, does any of 21 registered challengers — calibration-first variants, a Gaussian process, a small MLP, ensembles, and the 2025 TabPFN v2 tabular foundation model — earn a keep?
  2. The guard bar. Across a registered split lottery, does any challenger clear a joint selection guard over all model×size cells?
  3. The actionability bar. At any training size from 60 rows down to 8, is there a rung where a keep-sized win is even arithmetically possible — and if so, does anyone take it?

And one mechanism question behind all three: when something does win, is it the architecture — or is the same gain available by adjusting Fisher's own family?

The study's motto, written into the contract before any run: 「证据在擂台，封条管纪律。」 — "The arena is the evidence; the seal is the discipline."

The honesty inversion. This study cannot claim blindness and does not try to. Study 07 published its entire ledger, both floor sidecars, its measured floor and its one sealed value, so every one of these 100 flowers is public knowledge before this study begins. Its own scouting ledger says so in the first paragraph: this is a prospectively locked second study on fully scouted data, its sealed rows are procedurally fresh only — the protocol never conditions selection on them, but nobody is blind to them. The lock, not blindness, is the integrity claim. Everything below — seeds, bars, eligibility, branch sentences — was registered and committed before the first challenger ran.

Postscript — the harness learned this trick. The headroom audit this study ran by hand is now a first-class check in klein itself (v1.3): declare metric.bound.ideal and the harness computes h = (incumbent − ideal)/δ at preflight and verify, and klein run-one refuses development transactions on a keep-infeasible frontier until klein headroom ack records the closed door with a registered branch sentence — exactly the door-closed/door-ajar device pre-committed here. The floor's estimand must now be named too (marginal-resplit | paired-comparison): study 07's own lottery sidecar shows the paired spread exceeding the marginal for five of six families, so neither is "the sharper one" by default.

Method

Two frontiers, two sealed looks, one measured floor. The study declares a primary track (the anchor lane) and a challenger track, each scored on val_brier — inherited from study 07 so the two studies are comparable, and chosen for reasons worth repeating: AUC pegs at 1.000 on this pair (a ceiling cannot be beaten), takes only about 101 distinct values on 20 evaluation rows, and is invariant under monotone recalibration — which would have made the entire calibration lane of this roster indistinguishable from its raw versions and the mechanism question unanswerable. Log-loss was rejected in study 07 as a clipping-epsilon artifact.

The ledger floor. Study 07's exact recipe, re-run on the new split: twenty group-aware re-draws of the non-sealed rows, floor = of the anchor's development Brier across those draws, with a pre-registered raise-only escalation to klein's default . It measured 0.029.

The arena is the primary evidence, and it is a measurement sweep, not a ledger: ten seeded repeats of stratified group 4-fold over the 79 non-sealed rows, so every row is scored in development exactly ten times. Inside each fold the training pool is subsampled by a nested quota scan to each rung , and the same subset is served to every family — pairing is exact by construction, not by adjustment. The sweep runs in two stages, committed in order: Stage A fits the anchor and the control only and commits the headroom table before any challenger number exists; Stage B then runs the roster.

Open and closed rungs. Per rung, the floor is , where 0.005 is a fixed materiality floor — a registered constant, not an estimated noise quantity. A rung is OPEN when : the anchor's own mean error is at least as large as the bar, so a perfect oracle could clear it. Open is necessary and nowhere near sufficient. Closure carries a descriptive label — ceiling-closed when the anchor is simply too close to perfect, fog-closed when the split-lottery floor has outgrown the error.

The guard, named honestly. The study's Bar-1 is a selection guard, not a significance test. Per cell it forms the paired improvement over the anchor, summarises each repeat, and computes a t-statistic; one joint test then applies Rademacher sign flips at the repeat level — all 1024 enumerated — simultaneously across every cell in a fixed 113-cell family, and a cell clears when its max-t adjusted score is at most 0.05. Its registered status is written into the analysis file itself: this is a randomization diagnostic under a registered symmetry assumption, an assumption not derivable from the partition lottery alone, because every cell shares one anchor and the fits are deterministic given the partition. It is therefore never described as exact, never as family-wise error control, and never as a statement about irises in general. In this study, "detectable" always abbreviates "cleared this registered guard in this lottery."

Bar-2, the actionability bar, requires all three: the guard cleared, the mean gain at least , and the rung open. And the mechanism question gets a decision rule of its own: if the best LDA-family adjustment at a rung — two calibration maps and a covariance-shrinkage estimator — captures at least half of the best cleared challenger's gain, the claim for that rung downgrades. That ratio is reported as an observed quantity; it does not decompose mechanisms causally, and the study says so.

The sealed coda spends one look per track, and its role is written down before it is spent: a band-check, never estimation. The frozen analysis picks the branch mechanically and writes a manifest — families, baked training positions, hashes, numeric bands, and the sign convention — which the two pre-registered registry entries then read. No code is edited after selection. The band carries no nominal coverage once a winner has been selected against the same folds that produced it, and klein's confirmed label records protocol completion, not an evidence upgrade.

Data

The same 100 flowers as study 07, prepared by the same script, byte-identical: Fisher's hard pair — versicolor against virginica — four measurements at 0.1 cm print resolution, a 50/50 target, and two columns that are never features: the 3-class species code and the group_id.

The twins ruling, inherited. Hard-pair rows 51 and 92 (rows 102 and 143 of the printed table) carry identical measurements — 5.8, 2.7, 5.1, 1.9, both virginica — and study 07 established that the duplication is original to Fisher's own printed Table I. Duplicate entry cannot be distinguished from two genuinely identical flowers at that resolution, so the rows are not deleted: they share one group id and always travel together. Ninety-nine groups over one hundred rows.

The twins drew the sealed ticket. The pre-committed split seed put them behind the seal, together — the ruling held automatically, and it reshaped the study's geometry. The measured partition is 59 train / 20 development / 21 sealed (the group machinery's ±1 wobble, documented not forced), with a sealed base rate of 13 virginica to 8 versicolor — a group split is not stratified, and that is a consequence, not a defect. The arena therefore works over 79 rows containing no multi-row group at all, which made the subsampler's twins-last rule registered but idle for the whole study.

The split law applies inside the estimators too. This is the data lesson study 08 added. Calibration wrappers, the kNN tuner and the stacking meta-learner all run their own internal cross-validation, and by default that is row-level — which would let a multi-row group straddle an inner boundary and quietly violate the very ruling the outer split enforces. Every such estimator here receives precomputed, group-aware, stratified inner splits. For the same reason SVC(probability=True) — whose internal Platt calibration is row-level and not overridable — is banned in favour of an external calibration wrapper; scikit-learn 1.9 has since deprecated that parameter in favour of exactly the construction this study adopted. And every calibration wrapper runs with ensemble=False, so the base model is fit on all training rows and only the calibration map is learned out-of-fold: the pure calibration lane the mechanism question needs.

The external artifact. TabPFN's weights are the one thing in this study that did not come from the repository. They are pinned three ways — package version in the lockfile, checkpoint family selected in-factory rather than by the library's "auto" path, and offline mode exported for every study run so no fit can silently reach the network. A pre-gate spike on unrelated data confirmed bit-identical predictions across two fits at a fixed seed before the model was allowed onto the roster.

The Journey

Before the gates: a referee. The registered protocol went to an independent adversarial reviewer before any gate was recorded, and came back not gate-ready with nine blocking items. Three mattered most, and all three were adopted. The multiplicity procedure was demoted from "exact family-wise error control" to a registered selection guard under a stated assumption, and every downstream sentence about the word "detectable" was rewritten to match. The inner cross-validation of every calibrated, tuned and stacked estimator was found to be row-level — a genuine violation of this study's own twins ruling, invisible from the outside — and was replaced with group-aware splits. And the sealed coda, which had been described as choosing its wrapper families at the method gate, could not have done so honestly, because the winner does not exist until the arena has run; it was re-engineered to a committed manifest that pre-registered registry entries read at run time, so no code is edited after selection.

The predecessor's lesson, reproduced. Study 07's nineteenth finding reads: register seed schemes inside the numeric domain of the library that will consume them. Study 08's registered seed namespace exceeded that bound, and all twenty ledger-floor trials crashed on the identical error study 07 had already suffered. The crashed sidecar is preserved beside the good one, the namespace was amended inside the domain, the fix was committed before any floor was stated, and the gates were re-recorded. Reading a lesson is not the same as obeying it; the ledger is what caught the difference.

The floor, then the door. The measured floor came in at 0.029, and the anchor's development score on the fresh split was 0.029442. Their ratio — the declared headroom — is 1.015. For the first time across both studies it exceeded one: a keep was arithmetically possible, and a challenger needed only to reach 0.000442 to take it. The door was ajar. This was the roughly one-in-five branch, and it fired against the majority prior — which cost the study nothing, because both branches had been written out as sentences before E0001 ran. There was no redraw and no re-registration, because the contract forbids both.

The ladder was shut anyway. Stage A then measured what the door opened onto. At 60, 45, 30 and 20 training rows the rungs are ceiling-closed: the anchor sits between 0.017 and 0.028 from perfect while the bar sits above it. At 12 and 8 rows they are fog-closed: the anchor's error grows to 0.069 and 0.129, but the split-lottery floor grows faster still, to 0.147 and 0.252. The striking part is how stable the ratio is — about one-half at every rung. The incumbent's error and its own wobble under a change of split scale together, all the way down the ladder. Shrinking the data does not open the game; it fogs it.

The parade. Twenty-one challenger transactions ran on the declared split in registry order — 18 discards, 3 crashes, and no keeps. Three challengers did beat the anchor's raw score: 0.024709, 0.026958 and 0.028670 against the anchor's 0.029442. The best of them improved by 0.16× the floor on a door that stood open by 0.000442. Score-beating is cheap; floor-beating is the game.

What the crashes taught. Two crashes were a genuine property of a registered configuration: the Gaussian process fits converge — finite kernel, finite log-marginal-likelihood — but its probability integral returns non-finite values on near-separable fits, and the evaluator refuses to score non-finite probabilities. It failed at 60 and 45 training rows and scored normally at 30 and below: separability broke it, not scarcity. The soft-vote ensemble containing it died of the same wound. The third crash was mine: precomputed absolute inner splits cannot survive the refits a stacking meta-learner performs on row subsets. It was fixed before Stage B, with the lawfulness argument recorded (no multi-row group exists outside the seal in this study), the method gate was re-recorded — and the ledger row stands where it fell.

The seals. Both looks were spent under the committed manifest. The anchor's sealed score was 0.077176; against its registered band, that holds — at 82% of the band's width, inside but not comfortably. The challenger's sealed score was 0.066863, and the gap between them, +0.010313, fell outside the band the arena had predicted — a recorded miss against a pre-registered prediction. The direction favoured the 2025 model. Its size was 0.36× the floor, on a single look, with no coverage after selection. The study's pre-committed sentence for exactly this outcome was written before the seal was broken, and it is the sentence the record uses.

The ladder never opens

All six rungs closed: ceiling-closed at 60, 45, 30 and 20 training rows, fog-closed at 12 and 8. The ratio of the anchor's mean error to that rung's own floor stays near one-half at every rung — 0.52, 0.53, 0.53, 0.45, 0.47, 0.51. As the data shrinks, the incumbent gets worse and the bar for beating it gets worse faster. This is the study's central negative result, and it was measured and committed before any challenger number existed.

The one flicker, and where it points

Exactly one cell cleared the guard: a quadratic discriminant analysis at eight training rows, mean paired gain +0.0730. Three qualifiers travel with that sentence permanently, and all three were registered in advance. It is 0.29× that rung's floor — detectable, not actionable. The fold-level sensitivity exhibit fires zero cells, so the detection is sensitive to the choice of analysis unit and is reported as such. And the capture ratio is 0.98: the best LDA-family adjustment at that rung reaches +0.0711 of QDA's +0.0730. To within two percent, the one detectable improvement over Fisher's 1936 rule is available by adjusting Fisher's own family. Architecture never showed up. The 2025 foundation model landed mid-pack on the declared split, roughly twice the anchor's Brier, and cleared no guard cell at any rung.

The instrument was alive the whole time

The sepal-only positive control separates at every measurable rung, from t = 121.7 at 60 rows down to t = 5.8 at 8 rows, every adjusted score at most 0.002. A degradation of the size the protocol was built to see remains visible even where the fog closes the rung. "Nothing cleared the bar" is therefore a statement about the challengers, not about a blind instrument.

The seals

The anchor's level held on the fresh seal, inside its registered band at 82% of the band's width. The 1936-versus-2025 gap fell outside the arena's predicted band — a recorded miss against a pre-registered prediction, in the direction of the challenger, at 0.36× the floor, on one look, with no coverage after selection. Both outcomes were written as sentences before the seals were broken, and both sentences are in the record. That is the whole point of writing them first: a surprise costs you nothing if you have already decided what you would say.

Only priors tagged uninformed are scorable — everything study 07 measured is scouting for study 08 and is excluded by rule. Coding Advice

  1. Audit the headroom before you spend a challenger budget. One measured table — each rung's mean incumbent error against that rung's own floor — told this study, before a single challenger ran, that no training size could pay a keep. The 21-family parade then cost minutes and produced landing points instead of false hope. Compute how much room exists above your incumbent in floor units. If it is below one, re-scope the question; do not run the tournament and then discover the prize was never there.

  2. Pre-script the branch you are sure will not fire. The door-ajar branch carried roughly one-in-five odds and it fired. Because both branches existed as committed sentences, the surprise cost zero improvisation — and, more importantly, removed the temptation to redraw the split and try again. The contract that forbids the redraw is what makes the result worth reading.

  3. Call a guard a guard. The multiplicity procedure here is a randomization diagnostic under an assumption that the design does not supply. Naming it honestly cost one sentence; it also predicted its own weakness, because the fold-level sensitivity exhibit then failed to confirm the single detection. Publish the sensitivity exhibit beside the guard every time. If the verdict changes when the analysis unit changes, the reader deserves to know before you do.

  4. Your split law applies inside the estimators. A group-aware outer split proves nothing if the calibration wrapper, the hyper-parameter tuner and the stacking meta-learner all resplit rows internally. Pass precomputed group-aware splits into every one of them, and prefer external calibration over any estimator whose internal calibration you cannot control — the library itself is moving that way.

  5. Pass split policies, not split indices, into anything that refits. Absolute index lists are correct exactly once, on the data they were computed from; a stacking meta-learner refitting on row subsets will reject them, loudly if you are lucky. That crash cost one ledger row and taught the rule.

  6. Keep the crash rows. One hundred sixty arena crashes and three parade crashes are data, not mess. They located the Gaussian process's failure mode — separability, not scarcity — and they kept the guard family honest, because cells with no data occupy their slots as never-firing placeholders instead of quietly shrinking the multiplicity correction.

  7. Register seeds inside the numeric domain of the library that consumes them. This is study 07's lesson, reproduced verbatim by its own successor: twenty crashed trials on a bound that a comment in the previous study already warned about. Reading a lesson is not obeying it. Put the check in code, or you will pay for it twice.

The pattern under all seven: the discipline is portable even where the findings are not. Nothing here transfers to another dataset. The habit of measuring the bar before the contest, of writing both endings in advance, and of keeping the failures where a reviewer can find them — that transfers to every study you will run.

Next Steps

  1. Run the learning-curve arena where headroom actually exists. This study's central question — does a tabular foundation model earn its keep when data runs out? — was fog-bound here, because the incumbent's error and its own floor scale together on this pair. On a problem where the incumbent's error is many floor units above zero, the same ladder becomes measurable rather than closed. The candidates already in this repository are the motor-insurance frequency and severity studies and the hurricane return-level work.

  2. Give the Gaussian process a bounded kernel and a second chance. Its failure here was a probability-integral pathology on near-separable fits, not a statement about the method. Registering a bounded-kernel variant as a candidate would test whether the pathology is fixable inside the same guard — and the existing crash rows give it a pre-measured target to beat.

  3. Reuse the capture-ratio lane as a general pattern. For any claim of the form "the modern method wins", register the classical-family-adjustment lane first and report the observed capture ratio beside the headline. It costs three extra candidates and it is the difference between "a 2025 model beat a 1936 rule" and "the gain was 98% available by adjusting the 1936 rule". Both sentences can be true of the same numbers; only one of them is worth acting on.

  4. Put the headroom audit in the framework itself. The one table that made this study cheap is currently a study-local script. As a first-class step — run it at the metrology phase, publish it before any challenger — it would save every future study the cost of discovering a closed door from the inside.

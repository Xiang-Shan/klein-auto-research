The Question

Study 07 asked whether ninety years of method history could beat Fisher's 1936 rule on his own hard pair, and answered: no keep was earned. That answer had a hole in it, and the hole was arithmetic. Brier scores cannot go below zero, so the most any challenger could have gained over the 1936 anchor was the anchor's own score — and on that study's split-lottery mean, the anchor's score was 0.57× the floor that decides whether a difference is worth acting on. A keep-sized win was not merely unobserved. It was unavailable.

So this study re-asks the question with the arithmetic in the open, a roster three times larger, and a data ladder underneath it:

  1. The ledger bar. On a freshly drawn declared split, does any of 21 registered challengers — calibration-first variants, a Gaussian process, a small MLP, ensembles, and the 2025 TabPFN v2 tabular foundation model — earn a keep?
  2. The guard bar. Across a registered split lottery, does any challenger clear a joint selection guard over all model×size cells?
  3. The actionability bar. At any training size from 60 rows down to 8, is there a rung where a keep-sized win is even arithmetically possible — and if so, does anyone take it?

And one mechanism question behind all three: when something does win, is it the architecture — or is the same gain available by adjusting Fisher's own family?

The study's motto, written into the contract before any run: 「证据在擂台，封条管纪律。」 — "The arena is the evidence; the seal is the discipline."

The honesty inversion. This study cannot claim blindness and does not try to. Study 07 published its entire ledger, both floor sidecars, its measured floor and its one sealed value, so every one of these 100 flowers is public knowledge before this study begins. Its own scouting ledger says so in the first paragraph: this is a prospectively locked second study on fully scouted data, its sealed rows are procedurally fresh only — the protocol never conditions selection on them, but nobody is blind to them. The lock, not blindness, is the integrity claim. Everything below — seeds, bars, eligibility, branch sentences — was registered and committed before the first challenger ran.

The Journey

Before the gates: a referee. The registered protocol went to an independent adversarial reviewer before any gate was recorded, and came back not gate-ready with nine blocking items. Three mattered most, and all three were adopted. The multiplicity procedure was demoted from "exact family-wise error control" to a registered selection guard under a stated assumption, and every downstream sentence about the word "detectable" was rewritten to match. The inner cross-validation of every calibrated, tuned and stacked estimator was found to be row-level — a genuine violation of this study's own twins ruling, invisible from the outside — and was replaced with group-aware splits. And the sealed coda, which had been described as choosing its wrapper families at the method gate, could not have done so honestly, because the winner does not exist until the arena has run; it was re-engineered to a committed manifest that pre-registered registry entries read at run time, so no code is edited after selection.

The predecessor's lesson, reproduced. Study 07's nineteenth finding reads: register seed schemes inside the numeric domain of the library that will consume them. Study 08's registered seed namespace exceeded that bound, and all twenty ledger-floor trials crashed on the identical error study 07 had already suffered. The crashed sidecar is preserved beside the good one, the namespace was amended inside the domain, the fix was committed before any floor was stated, and the gates were re-recorded. Reading a lesson is not the same as obeying it; the ledger is what caught the difference.

The floor, then the door. The measured floor came in at 0.029, and the anchor's development score on the fresh split was 0.029442. Their ratio — the declared headroom — is 1.015. For the first time across both studies it exceeded one: a keep was arithmetically possible, and a challenger needed only to reach 0.000442 to take it. The door was ajar. This was the roughly one-in-five branch, and it fired against the majority prior — which cost the study nothing, because both branches had been written out as sentences before E0001 ran. There was no redraw and no re-registration, because the contract forbids both.

The ladder was shut anyway. Stage A then measured what the door opened onto. At 60, 45, 30 and 20 training rows the rungs are ceiling-closed: the anchor sits between 0.017 and 0.028 from perfect while the bar sits above it. At 12 and 8 rows they are fog-closed: the anchor's error grows to 0.069 and 0.129, but the split-lottery floor grows faster still, to 0.147 and 0.252. The striking part is how stable the ratio is — about one-half at every rung. The incumbent's error and its own wobble under a change of split scale together, all the way down the ladder. Shrinking the data does not open the game; it fogs it.

The parade. Twenty-one challenger transactions ran on the declared split in registry order — 18 discards, 3 crashes, and no keeps. Three challengers did beat the anchor's raw score: 0.024709, 0.026958 and 0.028670 against the anchor's 0.029442. The best of them improved by 0.16× the floor on a door that stood open by 0.000442. Score-beating is cheap; floor-beating is the game.

What the crashes taught. Two crashes were a genuine property of a registered configuration: the Gaussian process fits converge — finite kernel, finite log-marginal-likelihood — but its probability integral returns non-finite values on near-separable fits, and the evaluator refuses to score non-finite probabilities. It failed at 60 and 45 training rows and scored normally at 30 and below: separability broke it, not scarcity. The soft-vote ensemble containing it died of the same wound. The third crash was mine: precomputed absolute inner splits cannot survive the refits a stacking meta-learner performs on row subsets. It was fixed before Stage B, with the lawfulness argument recorded (no multi-row group exists outside the seal in this study), the method gate was re-recorded — and the ledger row stands where it fell.

The seals. Both looks were spent under the committed manifest. The anchor's sealed score was 0.077176; against its registered band, that holds — at 82% of the band's width, inside but not comfortably. The challenger's sealed score was 0.066863, and the gap between them, +0.010313, fell outside the band the arena had predicted — a recorded miss against a pre-registered prediction. The direction favoured the 2025 model. Its size was 0.36× the floor, on a single look, with no coverage after selection. The study's pre-committed sentence for exactly this outcome was written before the seal was broken, and it is the sentence the record uses.

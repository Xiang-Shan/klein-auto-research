Data

The same 100 flowers as study 07, prepared by the same script, byte-identical: Fisher's hard pair — versicolor against virginica — four measurements at 0.1 cm print resolution, a 50/50 target, and two columns that are never features: the 3-class species code and the group_id.

The twins ruling, inherited. Hard-pair rows 51 and 92 (rows 102 and 143 of the printed table) carry identical measurements — 5.8, 2.7, 5.1, 1.9, both virginica — and study 07 established that the duplication is original to Fisher's own printed Table I. Duplicate entry cannot be distinguished from two genuinely identical flowers at that resolution, so the rows are not deleted: they share one group id and always travel together. Ninety-nine groups over one hundred rows.

The twins drew the sealed ticket. The pre-committed split seed put them behind the seal, together — the ruling held automatically, and it reshaped the study's geometry. The measured partition is 59 train / 20 development / 21 sealed (the group machinery's ±1 wobble, documented not forced), with a sealed base rate of 13 virginica to 8 versicolor — a group split is not stratified, and that is a consequence, not a defect. The arena therefore works over 79 rows containing no multi-row group at all, which made the subsampler's twins-last rule registered but idle for the whole study.

The split law applies inside the estimators too. This is the data lesson study 08 added. Calibration wrappers, the kNN tuner and the stacking meta-learner all run their own internal cross-validation, and by default that is row-level — which would let a multi-row group straddle an inner boundary and quietly violate the very ruling the outer split enforces. Every such estimator here receives precomputed, group-aware, stratified inner splits. For the same reason SVC(probability=True) — whose internal Platt calibration is row-level and not overridable — is banned in favour of an external calibration wrapper; scikit-learn 1.9 has since deprecated that parameter in favour of exactly the construction this study adopted. And every calibration wrapper runs with ensemble=False, so the base model is fit on all training rows and only the calibration map is learned out-of-fold: the pure calibration lane the mechanism question needs.

The external artifact. TabPFN's weights are the one thing in this study that did not come from the repository. They are pinned three ways — package version in the lockfile, checkpoint family selected in-factory rather than by the library's "auto" path, and offline mode exported for every study run so no fit can silently reach the network. A pre-gate spike on unrelated data confirmed bit-identical predictions across two fits at a fixed seed before the model was allowed onto the roster.

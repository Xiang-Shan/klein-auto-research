Method

Two frontiers, two sealed looks, one measured floor. The study declares a primary track (the anchor lane) and a challenger track, each scored on val_brier — inherited from study 07 so the two studies are comparable, and chosen for reasons worth repeating: AUC pegs at 1.000 on this pair (a ceiling cannot be beaten), takes only about 101 distinct values on 20 evaluation rows, and is invariant under monotone recalibration — which would have made the entire calibration lane of this roster indistinguishable from its raw versions and the mechanism question unanswerable. Log-loss was rejected in study 07 as a clipping-epsilon artifact.

The ledger floor. Study 07's exact recipe, re-run on the new split: twenty group-aware re-draws of the non-sealed rows, floor = of the anchor's development Brier across those draws, with a pre-registered raise-only escalation to klein's default . It measured 0.029.

The arena is the primary evidence, and it is a measurement sweep, not a ledger: ten seeded repeats of stratified group 4-fold over the 79 non-sealed rows, so every row is scored in development exactly ten times. Inside each fold the training pool is subsampled by a nested quota scan to each rung , and the same subset is served to every family — pairing is exact by construction, not by adjustment. The sweep runs in two stages, committed in order: Stage A fits the anchor and the control only and commits the headroom table before any challenger number exists; Stage B then runs the roster.

Open and closed rungs. Per rung, the floor is , where 0.005 is a fixed materiality floor — a registered constant, not an estimated noise quantity. A rung is OPEN when : the anchor's own mean error is at least as large as the bar, so a perfect oracle could clear it. Open is necessary and nowhere near sufficient. Closure carries a descriptive label — ceiling-closed when the anchor is simply too close to perfect, fog-closed when the split-lottery floor has outgrown the error.

The guard, named honestly. The study's Bar-1 is a selection guard, not a significance test. Per cell it forms the paired improvement over the anchor, summarises each repeat, and computes a t-statistic; one joint test then applies Rademacher sign flips at the repeat level — all 1024 enumerated — simultaneously across every cell in a fixed 113-cell family, and a cell clears when its max-t adjusted score is at most 0.05. Its registered status is written into the analysis file itself: this is a randomization diagnostic under a registered symmetry assumption, an assumption not derivable from the partition lottery alone, because every cell shares one anchor and the fits are deterministic given the partition. It is therefore never described as exact, never as family-wise error control, and never as a statement about irises in general. In this study, "detectable" always abbreviates "cleared this registered guard in this lottery."

Bar-2, the actionability bar, requires all three: the guard cleared, the mean gain at least , and the rung open. And the mechanism question gets a decision rule of its own: if the best LDA-family adjustment at a rung — two calibration maps and a covariance-shrinkage estimator — captures at least half of the best cleared challenger's gain, the claim for that rung downgrades. That ratio is reported as an observed quantity; it does not decompose mechanisms causally, and the study says so.

The sealed coda spends one look per track, and its role is written down before it is spent: a band-check, never estimation. The frozen analysis picks the branch mechanically and writes a manifest — families, baked training positions, hashes, numeric bands, and the sign convention — which the two pre-registered registry entries then read. No code is edited after selection. The band carries no nominal coverage once a winner has been selected against the same folds that produced it, and klein's confirmed label records protocol completion, not an evidence upgrade.

How to read a registered journey

Both tracks are registered: each run is a pre-declared cell, measured or crash, never a keep or a discard. So there is no frontier to climb. The “journey” is the sequence of cells, the two crashes that interrupted one of them, and the separate sealed confirmation cell. The step line of the engine's trajectory plot is empty by design; what matters is where the squares, the crosses and the star fall.

Development evidence (orders 8–11) and sealed confirmation evidence (orders 12–13) are different partitions and are labelled separately everywhere below.

The search track

One development cell measured the annealer (E0001), and one sealed cell measured it again on the sealed orders, once, after a dry run (E0005). Nothing about the mutable surface changed between them: the notary recorded an empty candidate diff on both, and section 6 shows the same bytes under both run ids.

The llm track, and the two crashes

The model arm needed four attempts at one development cell. E0002 died on an HTTP 429 rate limit from the provider after 613 calls. The transport was made to wait out rate limits and the concurrency was lowered; E0003 then died on a bare connection reset after 523 live calls plus 613 answered from E0002's shard. The transport was made to retry dropped connections, and the same pre-registered cell ran to completion as E0004. Neither crash was a bug in the ruler logic, both are kept in the ledger as crashes with an NA metric, and each fix was a dated lead decision with the contract re-recorded. No registered prediction changed.

The caveat this leaves is that E0004 answered a large share of its requests from the crashed runs' shards. Those are genuine recorded responses to byte-identical requests, but they make E0004 partly the same sample as the crashed runs, so only the salted re-query is a fully independent second draw (claim C12).

The ledger

The ledger key line names each track's metric and direction. The rows with an NA metric are the two crashes; the “measured” rows are the registered cells. The DRYRUN rows in aux_metrics.tsv and the files under sweeps/ are sealed rehearsals and are never evidence.

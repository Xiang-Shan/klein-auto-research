What to try next

These are the four next experiments from the findings, in priority order. Each is the control that converts a specific hypothesis above into a finding or a refutation.

  1. A contamination control, first. Re-run the LLM arm unchanged on instances with no public answer at the same 2000 evaluations: for example a perturbed Golomb variant with a forbidden difference set, whose optima the verifier can certify by exhaustive enumeration at small . A match there could not be retrieval. This is the single experiment that turns C7 and C8 into findings or refutations.
  2. A no-feedback arm. Resample the same model and prompt for 200 proposals without verdicts, on orders 8 and 11, registered as a rule on first_optimal_proposal. If the late matches recur without feedback they are resampling; if they vanish, feedback steered them.
  3. A compute-honest classical baseline. Run the annealer, or the hybrid of ref:dotu2005, at the LLM arm’s wall-clock or at 10 and 100 times the evaluations, reporting the budget in evaluations and seconds. This places the annealer’s zero on its own budget curve.
  4. Snapshot and temperature replication. Query a second dated snapshot at temperature 0, declare a price block (C13), keep the replica’s per-pair table (C14), and report results in orders as the unit (C11).

The study did not do any of these: the contract registered exactly two arms and both phase allowances were spent. The process lessons C3, C10 to C13 and C15 are candidates for promotion into the framework’s cross-study notes after a survey; that promotion is not done here.

## Group policy

- **Unit of leakage: the TEMPLATE.** A twin shares problem structure and wording with its original
  and with the other twins of its template, so a template straddling partitions is the leak a
  naive audit would flag. The index declares no `group` column; the `template` column (`tNNNN`)
  is that group. The design deliberately puts the original in `train`, the development twins in
  `development`, and the sealed twins in `test`; the straddle is the experiment, not a defect.
- The mechanized `group-overlap` row reads N/A (no group column). By hand: all 50 templates
  appear in all three template blocks (1 original, 4 twin_dev, 4 twin_sealed each); the 300
  `gsm8k` items carry no template and share 0 ids with the sources.
- Consequence: a bootstrap on twin blocks must resample whole templates (`lib/measure.py`,
  `item_bootstrap(..., groups=...)`); public GSM8K items are independent (bar the N5 pair), so an
  item bootstrap is right there.

## Contamination audit

**Publication date versus cutoff.** GSM8K (Cobbe et al., arXiv:2110.14168) was released in
October 2021. The model is `qwen-flash` on DashScope, pinned by the lead to the dated snapshot
`qwen-flash-2025-07-28`; its training cutoff is not published to this study. Five years of open
circulation make GSM8K-in-pretraining the working assumption, but a release earlier than the cutoff
is not proof of contamination and a later one would not prove cleanliness; neither is established
here. The probe measures it behaviourally.

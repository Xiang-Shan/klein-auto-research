Next steps & references

What to try next

  1. Make the direct arm direct, then re-measure the gain. A new study with an enforced bare-number arm (a token cap of a few tokens, or a response-format constraint) and compliance itself as a registered prediction, on the same public items and twin blocks. It is the cheapest test of whether the ceiling belongs to the instrument or to the model.
  2. Move off the ceiling. Repeat the paired comparison on items where the direct arm scores well below 0.95 (longer chains, harder re-instantiations) so that a registered gain of 0.10 is reachable.
  3. Power the contamination probe. Roughly four times the templates, with magnitude-matched twins, and the direct arm also run on the originals. Register the gap rule on the interval’s upper end, not the point estimate.
  4. Measure serving noise as the floor. At least three salted re-queries of one cell, recorded as fit noise. This study’s minimum deltas are pre-registered replication tolerances, not measured floors.

Prompting and evaluation-harness advice

The change. A step-by-step instruction in place of a number-only instruction, scored by a fixed integer-extraction rule. The model and date. qwen-flash-2025-07-28, a hosted snapshot, queried 2026-11-03 at temperature 0, thinking off, 1024-token cap. The item set and its status. Public GSM8K (presumed contaminated) and procedurally generated twins. The paired effect. Indistinguishable from zero on every block. The cost. Roughly twice the output tokens; no price pinned. What this does not say. Nothing about another model, another snapshot or a thinking-mode configuration.

The executed source, in three layers

The harness has three layers, and keeping them apart is the first piece of advice. The instrument (prompts, decoding parameters, scorer) is library code outside the mutable surface, written before any prediction was registered and then left alone. The measurement machinery (the lanes, the intervals, the table writer) is also outside the surface. The cell, analyze.py, is the only mutable file, and it only chooses which items to measure and how to summarize them.

Answer: marker. That is a sensible rule for chain-of-thought, and it is also exactly why a direct arm that ignores its

“direct-only” discordant items, and the study reports how many.

Eight lessons, each with the run that paid for it



    

  1. Audit the direct arm’s compliance from the ledger before reading any gain [C9]. The share of bare-number replies and
output tokens per item, against the CoT arm. Here, 4 of 300 replies were bare; the direct arm’s median output was
112.5 tokens against 243. (E0002.)
    2. 

  2. Check the headroom before you register a gain threshold [C10]. A gain cannot exceed one minus the direct score;
at a direct score of 0.95 a registered gain of 0.10 could not be reached. Measure the direct score first, from a pilot or from items off the ceiling. (E0002.)
    3. 

  3. Size a template-paired contamination probe to the gap it must resolve [C11]. Fifty templates gave interval widths of 0.075 and
0.105, so a gap of 0.05 could be neither detected nor excluded. Roughly four times the templates halves the interval. (E0003, E0006.)
    4. 

  4. Treat cells that share ledger entries as one measurement [C12]. The ledger’s de-duplication makes a study cheap and
replayable; it also couples the cells, and the findings must say which. (E0006.)
    5. 

  5. Never count a replay as a replication [C13]. The offline replay reproduced with a difference of 0 because it re-scored the same
recorded answers; the salted re-query changed 9 of 600 item-arm scores and moved the gain by 0.0167.
    6. 

  6. On twin blocks, report the cluster bootstrap over templates, not McNemar’s test [C14]. Four twins of one template are not
independent; McNemar’s p of 0.125 on the development twins overstates the evidence.
    7. 

  7. Expect a sign flip when the effect is below the serving noise. Temperature 0 on a hosted model is not determinism. One flipped
original is worth 0.02 of em_gap on 50 templates, so a handful of flips reverses a gap this small.
    8. 

  8. Rate limits are a harness fault, not a result. The first anchor run crashed on an HTTP rate-limit after almost all calls were
pinned; the pinned shard made the re-run nearly free. Lower the worker count on a shared account and let the ledger’s cache do the rest.
    9. 




Pitfalls and war stories



    

  • The identifier that looks like a number. The digit in the benchmark name GSM8K is read by the claim-sentence numeral scan as a
measured value. The claims lock carries it under a labelled alias whose note says it is an identifier, never a measurement. The tutorial does not render that alias.
    • 

  • Pinning the dated snapshot, not the alias. A provider can repoint an alias; the ledger records the model id actually served on every call,
and every record here names the same snapshot.
    • 

  • The positive control is one seeded plant. It tests the pairing and interval code, its point recovery is exact by construction, and it is not a power analysis.
    • 

  • The instrument was fixed before the predictions, and stayed fixed after the surprise. The lead kept the registered prompt after seeing the replies, so the
non-compliance is a property of the measurement, recorded as such.
    • 

  • A seed comment that was wrong. The synthesis script’s comment about independent seed streams was inaccurate (one positive-control seed equals one
negative-control repeat seed). The streams drive different operations, so no result moved, and the committed table was not regenerated.

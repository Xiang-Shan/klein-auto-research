The question

You run LLM benchmarks. A colleague proposes adding “think step by step” to the prompt for a cheap hosted model, and quotes the large gains that chain-of-thought (CoT) prompting produced on grade-school arithmetic a few years ago. Before you change the prompt for every downstream evaluation, two questions have to be answered on your model: does the step-by-step instruction raise exact-match on GSM8K, and does any such gain survive on items the model cannot have seen? GSM8K has been public for years, so a score on it may partly be a memory test. This study asks both, on one dated snapshot, qwen-flash-2025-07-28, queried at temperature 0 with its thinking mode off.

What was decided, and how it was registered

Two registered tracks, each a set of pre-declared measurement cells rather than a search (no cell was ever “kept” against an incumbent):

  • cot_gain — the paired difference in exact-match, CoT arm minus direct arm, on the same items. Registered prediction rules asked for a gain of at least on public items, on development twins and on sealed twins.
  • contamination — the template-paired gap between a GSM8K original and the mean over its four procedurally generated twins, CoT arm only. The registered rule asked for a gap of at most .

Each track declared that its headline would need a sealed confirmation cell and a replication before it could be called confirmed. How far the records got is shown in the evidence block of section 5, not restated here.

What the study found, before the method

  • No CoT gain was detected anywhere. The paired gain was +0.01 on public items (95% interval [-0.01, 0.03]), -0.025 on development twins ([-0.065, 0.005]) and 0.000 on sealed twins ([-0.055, 0.045]). Every interval excludes the registered 0.10.
  • No contamination signature was detected, but a 5-point gap was not excluded. The template-paired gap was 0.02 on the development twins and 0.01 on the sealed twins; the upper interval ends were 0.06 and 0.065, above the 0.05 bound.
  • The “direct” arm was not direct. Told to answer with the number only, the model wrote its working anyway, so the comparison is CoT against a terse CoT. That, and a near-ceiling score on every block, is the most useful thing to take from the study.

Scope of every sentence on this page: one model snapshot, two registered prompt wordings, one query date. Nothing here says anything about another model, a thinking-mode configuration or another prompt.

The data story

Shape

The source is the bundled GSM8K test file, verified by hash before preparation. The study uses four blocks of an index that the contract names, so no evaluator invents its own partition:

BlockSplitWhat it is
originaltrainthe 50 GSM8K test items the templates were written from
gsm8kdevelopment300 further public items (the anchor sample)
twin_devdevelopment4 twins per template, generator seed A (200 items)
twin_sealedtest, sealed4 twins per template, a second generator seed (200 items), opened once per track

The unit of independence on a twin block is the template, so the effective sample for the contamination track is the 50 templates, not the 200 twins.

The gotchas that travelled into the findings

  • The twins are not perfectly faithful. Twin answers run larger than their originals’ (a median ratio above one on both blocks, driven by a few templates), which pushes a measured gap upward. The study registered a magnitude-matched sensitivity cell for that.
  • Five templates are public elsewhere. GSM-Symbolic publishes number-swapped variants of five of the fifty source items, so for those five a “fresh” twin is less fresh. The contamination cells report a five-versus-forty-five split, descriptively.
  • The sealed block is not structurally independent of the development block. It reuses the same fifty templates; most sealed twins share a 13-gram with a development twin, and a few repeat a (template, answer) pair. The sealed cells therefore guard against luck in twin instances, not against the choice of templates.
  • Small answer spaces. A dozen templates have fewer than four distinct answers among their four twins; the twin is then a weaker independent draw.
  • The model’s training cutoff is not published. That GSM8K is in pretraining is a working assumption, which the probe tests behaviourally rather than assumes.

The go/no-go call

The mechanized near-duplicate check failed by design: a twin shares a 13-gram with its own template’s original and twins, which is exactly what a twin is. The auditor showed that every hit is own-template and none touches another template or any public item. Under the protocol any mechanized FAIL is a blocker, so the card stayed at no-go until the lead recorded a gate override with that reasoning, the actor, and the index hash. That override is on the record, not a clean pass, and the findings carry it as a caveat. The audit itself made no model call.

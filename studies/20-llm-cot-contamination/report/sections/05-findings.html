Findings & insights

The records below are copied by the builder from the claims lock and the study state; the prose after them cites them and never restates a total.

The verdicts

  • [C2] and [C4] — no CoT gain. The paired gain over the registered direct prompt was +0.01 (public, [-0.01, 0.03]), -0.025 (development twins) and 0.000 (sealed twins); every interval excludes the registered 0.10. The salted re-query moved the public gain to -0.0067, inside the 0.03 tolerance.
  • [C3] — no contamination signature at this instrument’s resolution. The gaps were 0.02 (development) and 0.01 (sealed), and the interval widths, 0.075 and 0.105, are too wide to detect or exclude a 5-point gap. “None detected” is not “none present”.
  • [C1] — the direct arm scored 0.95, 0.985 and 0.97 on public, development-twin and sealed-twin items, far above the 0.55 to 0.85 the card expected.

The surprise: the “direct” arm is a terse chain of thought

[C5] The registered number-only instruction was not followed: only 4 of 300 public replies were a bare number, the direct arm’s median output was 112.5 tokens against 243 for CoT, and CoT wrote only 1.85 times the direct arm’s mean output tokens on public items (1.92 on sealed twins). What was compared is CoT against a terse CoT, not CoT against answering without written steps. The method card named this failure mode in advance; its quantitative form, CoT writing at least twenty times as many tokens, was the most informative prior that missed.

Why the gain is hard to see: the ceiling

[C6, re-scoped by erratum E1] Both arms score between 0.95 and 0.985 in every cell, so the largest gain the public cell could have shown is one minus the direct score, 0.05. The gain was measured, with a tight interval; what the ceiling removed is headroom, and a gain as large as 0.10 was unreachable given the observed direct scores. Whether a compliant direct arm would reopen that headroom is an untested hypothesis, not a finding.

What the contamination probe saw, template by template

[C7, exploratory] The sign of a gap this small is set by serving noise on 50 originals: in the E0003 re-query three originals went from right to wrong and one from wrong to right, moving the originals’ score from 0.98 to 0.94. [C8, exploratory, unresolved beyond “noise”] The development-twin reversal (direct ahead by 0.025) did not survive on the sealed twins, where the arms tied, and the discordant twins sit in a few templates, which the cluster interval absorbs and McNemar does not.

Controls added after the first referee round (post-hoc, exploratory)

The headline results are nulls, so the record has to show that the scorer and interval code would have seen a gain if one existed. Neither control was registered; both were computed offline from the committed ledger and tables, with no model call.

  • Negative control [C16]. Re-scoring the recorded replies against golds permuted across each cell’s items drops exact-match to chance, a mean of 0.0126 for the direct arm over 100 permutations of the public items, and leaves the CoT gain at 0.0033 on the public items and 0.000 on both twin blocks. The pipeline does not manufacture a gain from mismatched answers.
  • Positive control [C17]. A gain of 0.10 planted by scoring a seeded set of correct direct replies as wrong was recovered exactly in all three cells, with an interval wholly above zero in each (public [0.0733, 0.15], sealed twins [0.045, 0.15]). A planted 0.05 was detected on the public items but not on either twin block. Each plant is a single seeded realization per block, never a power estimate (erratum E2): the fair reading is “one seeded 0.05 plant was not detected on either twin block”, and the detection limit is better read from the twin-block interval widths.

What the two controls do not do: neither plants an effect at the reply level, so neither can show that the scorer would credit a real CoT advantage that extraction might mangle. That part rests on the instrument check (the re-scored exact-match equals the notarized value in every cell and arm) and on the offline replay.

Multiple comparisons

[C18, post-hoc] With 4 comparisons in the cot_gain family and 2 in the contamination family, Bonferroni-level intervals (levels 0.9875 and 0.975) still exclude the registered 0.10 for every gain cell (upper ends 0.0367 public, 0.01 development twins, 0.055 sealed twins) and still fail to exclude 0.05 for either gap (upper ends 0.065 and 0.075). The families were drawn by the synthesist after the fact; the next test study should fix the family and guard at consultation.

What changes in practice

  • Change measured: a step-by-step instruction against a number-only instruction, temperature 0, 1024-token cap, thinking off.
  • Model and date: qwen-flash-2025-07-28, queried on 2026-11-03; every ledger record names that snapshot as served. One model snapshot only.
  • Items and contamination status: 300 public items (presumed seen), 200 development twins and 200 sealed twins of 50 templates.
  • Paired effect: +0.01 [-0.01, 0.03] public; -0.025 [-0.065, 0.005] development twins; 0.000 [-0.055, 0.045] sealed twins.
  • Token cost per item: mean output tokens 265.7 (CoT) against 143.5 (direct) on public items, 295.4 against 154.1 on sealed twins. No price is pinned, so no dollar figure is given.
  • Reading: for this snapshot, on GSM8K-style arithmetic, the explicit step-by-step instruction bought no detectable exact-match over the registered direct instruction while roughly doubling output tokens. No equivalence margin was registered, so that is not a demonstration that the two are equal; and it is not evidence that written steps are unnecessary, because the direct arm wrote them too.

Caveats that limit every conclusion

  • One snapshot, two prompt wordings, one date.
  • Non-compliance. The direct arm is a terse CoT; the first two predictions measure the instruction.
  • Coupled cells. The two sealed cells share ledger entries, and E0004’s CoT arm is E0003’s twin answers.
  • The twin-gain replication is track-level. The salted re-query re-asked the public items, not the twins.
  • A sign flip under re-query, and a supported sealed-gap rule whose interval upper end passes the bound.
  • The data gate passed by recorded override, not a clean pass.
  • The controls and family-wise guard are post-hoc; a replay satisfies no confirmation requirement.

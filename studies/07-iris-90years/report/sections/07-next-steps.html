Predictions to falsify: δ moves by less than 0.005, and a stratified lottery gives a smaller anchor std than the group lottery — which would make the group-aware floor the conservative choice, as intended.

Predictions: the mean paired delta stays within ±0.008 of +0.023445, and the fraction of draws inside ±δ stays near 13/20. RQ2 then becomes a study about the verdict quantity rather than about petals.
  3. The ladder as a function of n. Refit the seven families on nested group-aware subsamples (train 20/40/60, same sealed rows frozen out) and measure δ at each size. Prediction: δ shrinks roughly as 1/√n and the first challenger to clear it is SVM-RBF, not HGBT — its lottery paired spread (std 0.012015) is under a third of HGBT's (0.040144). If HGBT crosses first, the "a closed form has no fitting variance to spend" mechanism is weaker than the method card claims.

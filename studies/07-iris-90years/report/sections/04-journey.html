The Journey

Nine ledger transactions, every one still resolvable to its exact candidate commit: one keep, one registered crash, seven discards (the sealed look is a discard by design — sealed evidence never enters the adaptive frontier). The whole run, end to end, cost ≈ 9.2 s of compute. The two exhibits below carry the story: the lottery that priced the floor, and the ladder the floor judged.

Metrology before combat. E0001 anchored the frontier at 0.026744.

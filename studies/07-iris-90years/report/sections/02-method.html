The Method

The 1936 anchor, taught. You have two clouds of flowers in four-dimensional measurement space. Project every flower onto one direction — a weighted sum of the four measurements — and the problem collapses to one dimension. Fisher's criterion picks the direction as a signal-to-noise ratio: maximize the squared gap between the projected class means divided by the within-class spread along that same direction,

where the within-class scatter sums squared deviations about each class mean. Only the direction is determined, which is why any comparison against printed 1936 coefficients must be a cosine, not a difference. The consequence this study leans on: there is nothing random in it — one mean per class, one scatter matrix, one linear solve. The METHOD gate proved the ten-line from-scratch implementation and sklearn's LDA are the same object: cosine ≥ 1 − 1e-12 on all three solvers (the svd residual is one float64 ULP).

The instrument. klein's lifecycle as used here: three recorded gates before any run (CONSULT freezes the contract by hash; DATA writes the provenance and leakage audit; METHOD proves the anchor from scratch), then an append-only ledger where every candidate is committed before it executes, then one sealed look. Because a 2026-08-24 design panel had already measured this same dataset, the study adds a scouting ledger, committed before the first gate — disclosure, not blindness. Its own words: "We scouted first, and everything we scouted is on the record. The registered predictions only count for the part we did not scout." A fresh split seed buys exactly one thing, stated no wider: this partition, and therefore these 20 sealed rows, were never scored during scouting.

The floor, measured not guessed. The protocol-prescribed k-seed fit-noise sweep ran first and returned std exactly 0.0 over 5 seeds — registered in advance, because a closed-form estimator gives a seed nothing to perturb. It is not a confidence interval and not a significance test; the registered estimand is a protocol-level decision claim, nothing more.

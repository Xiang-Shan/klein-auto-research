The Question

Every pricing actuary has run this argument: the production rater is a GLM; the data-science team says gradient boosting prices the book better. On freMTPL2 — 678,013 French motor policies, 26,406 claims — how much better is it really, measured in the loss both models actually minimize (Poisson deviance), and with an honest error bar that says when a difference is weather rather than climate?

Three registered questions: does HGBT beat a plain GLM beyond the measured floor (RQ1)? does classical GLM feature-shaping close most of the gap (RQ2)? does native categorical handling matter versus one-hot at this cardinality (RQ3)? Priors were written down first, sourced from this repo's own knowledge cards — and one of them lost.

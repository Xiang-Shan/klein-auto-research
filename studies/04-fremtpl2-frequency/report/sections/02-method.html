The Method

Claim frequency is a rate. The clean formulation both contenders share: fit on y = ClaimNb/Exposure with sample_weight = Exposure — exactly equivalent to the offset formulation, and natively supported by PoissonRegressor and HistGradientBoostingRegressor(loss="poisson"). Score with exposure-weighted mean Poisson deviance, d(y, μ) = 2(y log(y/μ) − y + μ), on a fixed development fold.

The methodological star of this study is the noise floor. It turned out to be three different numbers: the fit-seed spread of the boosted model (2σ = 0.0004), the marginal bootstrap SE of the fold's mean deviance (2×SE = 0.0108), and — the right one for a comparison — the paired-difference bootstrap SE (2×SE = 0.0018), where both models are scored on the same resampled rows so their shared shot noise cancels. The keep bar was set to the paired floor.

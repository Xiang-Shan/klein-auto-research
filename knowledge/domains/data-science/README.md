# Data science and causal inference

Seeded 2026-11-03 by study 23 (`23-lalonde-causal-replication`: a replication of Dehejia and Wahba's NSW propensity-score analysis against the experimental benchmark, PSID-1 as the development comparison group and CPS-1 as the sealed one). Doctrine anchor: name the estimand before the estimator, and keep predictive and causal claims in separate classes. Report an observational contrast beside the naive comparison and, where one exists, an experimental benchmark. Overlap diagnostics and specification sensitivity are part of the evidence. Every estimate carries a named interval. Multiple specifications are a family, or they stay exploratory.

**Scope of every line below.** The seeding study ran one propensity specification per estimator, a 500-draw percentile bootstrap that holds the 260 experimental controls fixed, and four PSID-1 cells with no family-wise adjustment. Everything it says about PSID-1 estimators is exploratory. Its confirmed lines are the reproduction values and one sealed CPS-1 cell. All values are 1978 earnings in 1982 dollars.

## Typed citations

- **Get a benchmark if one exists at all, because the naive contrast can miss by more than the effect itself.** Against PSID-1, the naive difference in means was -15204.78 against the experimental 1794.34: the wrong sign, and a miss larger than the benchmark. Against the sealed CPS-1 lane it was -8497.52. LaLonde's 1986 lesson reproduces in one row. `(supports 23-lalonde-causal-replication#C2)`
- **Several estimators on one propensity score give a range, not a set of candidate answers.** With the Dehejia-Wahba logit on PSID-1, IPW gave 3038.44, AIPW 2683.82 and 1-NN matching 560.28. The spread from lowest to highest is larger than the experimental 1794.34. Only AIPW landed within the registered 1000 dollars of the benchmark (gap +889.48), and the study's bootstrap ranges cannot rank the three. Report such a set as "the estimated ATT lies between these values depending on the estimator". `(supports 23-lalonde-causal-replication#C31)` `(supports 23-lalonde-causal-replication#C23)`
- **Print the weight-concentration pair before the point estimate, and read it against the pool size and the pre-weighting balance.** The pair is the largest single weight share and the number of comparison units used. On PSID-1 the common-support trim kept 1146 of 2490 comparison men, and one man carried 0.135 of the odds-weight mass. Matching used 56 distinct men, one of them matched to a 0.146 share of the trainees. On sealed CPS-1 the largest share was 0.0426. This bears out the repository seed card `causal-propensity-methods.md`: under poor overlap a few heavily weighted units dominate the estimate. `(supports 23-lalonde-causal-replication#C7)` `(supports 23-lalonde-causal-replication#C15)` `(see 23-lalonde-causal-replication erratum E3)`
- **Opposite-signed misses by two estimators on one score are a hypothesis about which few units are heavy, not evidence of a shared bias.** IPW and matching missed by +1244.09 and -1234.06. The study's reading is that each counterfactual rests on a different handful of PSID-1 men. Per-man weights were not pinned, so the reading is unresolved. `(supports 23-lalonde-causal-replication#C11)`
- **The answer depends on the specification, and a "drop one covariate" cell must drop only that covariate.** Replacing the logit with a specification that removed all three 1974-earnings terms and added a 1975 zero-earnings flag moved the PSID-1 IPW estimate from 3038.44 to -15.33. It also moved the common support from 1146 to 1092 men and the largest weight share from 0.135 to 0.0583. The shift belongs to that whole change, not to 1974 earnings alone. Hold the support fixed, or report the support change beside the shift. `(supports 23-lalonde-causal-replication#C22)`
- **Write a sensitivity prediction against the baseline cell, not against the benchmark band.** IPW missed the 1000-dollar band both with the 1974-earnings terms and without them, so the registered rule "the no-1974 IPW misses by more than $1000" would have been supported even if 1974 earnings were irrelevant. A rule stated against the baseline cell discriminates. The rule stated against the band could not. `(supports 23-lalonde-causal-replication#C13)` `(see 23-lalonde-causal-replication erratum E1)`
- **Say what the interval is for.** A percentile bootstrap that re-fits the score but holds the experimental controls fixed is an interval for the ATT estimate, not for its gap to the benchmark. For 1-NN matching it is descriptive only. Resample the controls, or use an analytic variance, before quoting coverage of a benchmark. The study's ranges each contained 1794.34, and no inference rests on that. `(supports 23-lalonde-causal-replication#C17)` `(supports 23-lalonde-causal-replication#C23)`
- **A registered selection among estimators is a tie-break, not a ranking.** AIPW won on PSID-1 at +889.48 against +1244.09 and -1234.06. The study's own bootstrap cannot test that order, and only the sealed cell speaks for the selected estimator, once. `(supports 23-lalonde-causal-replication#C24)`
- **Agreement with the benchmark on a sealed comparison group is consistency, not validation of selection on observables.** Carried to sealed CPS-1 with its covariate list unchanged and its models re-fit, AIPW estimated an ATT of 1327.54, bootstrap interval [-129.17, 2835.62], a gap of -466.80. The interval includes zero. Better CPS-1 overlap is only one candidate reason why the sealed gap differs from the development gap: pre-weighting balance points both ways, and one sealed draw cannot separate any mechanism from luck. `(supports 23-lalonde-causal-replication#C29)` `(supports 23-lalonde-causal-replication#C28)` `(see 23-lalonde-causal-replication erratum E7)`
- **Cross-read every published target against the paper's other tables before registering a replication.** In the seeding study, 9 of 11 development targets and 3 of 5 sealed CPS-1 targets reproduced within one dollar. The four misses were exactly the comparison-group adjusted estimates and their standard errors. Note a's covariate list reproduces Table 2 Panel C column (4) on all three lanes, and neither all-covariates reading recovers the printed Table 3 comparison values. Reading Table 3 beside Table 2 at METHOD explained both reproduction refutations before any cell ran. The reading that Table 3 column (2) mixes two Table 2 columns is exploratory, because every target was transcribed from the NBER working paper alone. `(supports 23-lalonde-causal-replication#C30)` `(supports 23-lalonde-causal-replication#C26)` `(supports 23-lalonde-causal-replication#C14)` `(supports 23-lalonde-causal-replication#C10)`

## Open questions this domain inherits

- Does a placebo outcome (1975 earnings as the outcome, with the 1975 terms removed from the score) come out near zero on both comparison groups? A benchmark only says whether an estimate happened to agree. A placebo tests the identification assumption, and it is the seeding study's first §⑦ item.
- Does the CPS-1 agreement survive a registered specification family, crossed with IPW, AIPW and matching and guarded family-wise? Does a cell that drops only the three 1974 terms, with the support held at the baseline's, show 1974 earnings to be load-bearing?
- What covariate set produced Table 2 Panel C column (5)? A second source (the JASA text) or a registered sweep over candidate lists would settle it.

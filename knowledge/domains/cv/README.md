# Computer vision

Seeded 2026-11-03 by study 21 (`21-bloodmnist-cnn`: an 8-class blood-cell classifier on 28 x 28 BloodMNIST images, trained from scratch on CPU as three-seed arrays). Doctrine anchor: split by the unit that would leak, never by file, and audit near-duplicates across partitions before any model trains. Score imbalanced multiclass work by macro-F1 or balanced accuracy, with accuracy reported beside it and the confusion table pinned. Deep runs are seed arrays with a measured floor. Augmentation happens inside the training loop. Keep a benchmark's official split and say whether it is patient-grouped.

**Scope of every line below.** Every comparison between development configurations in the seeding study is exploratory. Only its sealed level, the one sealed run plus a replication, is confirmed. Every score is image-level, because the official MedMNIST split is not grouped by patient or smear.

## Typed citations

- **Before calling a batch-normalization candidate a failure, compare its training-mode and evaluation-mode outputs and recompute the running statistics on the training set.** In the seeding study the first BN candidate collapsed to macro-F1 0.3005. The same layers, with running statistics recomputed on the training set before evaluation, scored +0.5955 higher, and that was the only single change to earn a keep (+0.1771 over the anchor). The study's explanation is that momentum-0.1 running averages lag weights still moving at a constant learning rate. That explanation is a hypothesis resting on an off-loop diagnostic, not on evidence. The advice does not depend on it. `(supports 21-bloodmnist-cnn#C12)` `(supports 21-bloodmnist-cnn#C7)`
- **Measure fit noise across seed blocks as well as item noise before the adaptive phase.** At the anchor, the block-to-block spread of the three-seed mean (sd 0.0472) was 3.94 times the item-bootstrap sd of 0.0120 that set the keep bar. The anchor's own block was the highest of four. When fit noise exceeds the item-noise bar, read every keep together with its seed spread, not against the bar alone. The repository's seed card `cnn-image-classification.md` expected item noise to dominate. This is the line it should learn. `(supports 21-bloodmnist-cnn#C8)` `(supports 21-bloodmnist-cnn#C13)`
- **The recipe CNN barely beat a pixel-linear softmax, so always run the linear baseline.** Flatten plus one linear layer reached development macro-F1 0.6903, far above the chance controls. The recipe two-block CNN cleared it by only +0.0286, inside the keep bar of 0.0438. The study reads the anchor as optimisation-limited rather than capacity-limited: the same network with working BN at the same 1500 steps reached 0.8960, and its seed sd fell from 0.0255 to 0.0145. That reading is an exploratory interpretation. `(supports 21-bloodmnist-cnn#C1)` `(supports 21-bloodmnist-cnn#C2)` `(supports 21-bloodmnist-cnn#C11)`
- **Budget the ablations before registering a multi-lever bundle.** The four-lever bundle (a third block, working BN, flip/rotation augmentation, 4000 steps) kept at 0.9461, +0.0501 over its incumbent. Its margins over the depth-only and schedule-only runs (+0.0175 and +0.0221) were inside the bar, so its gain cannot be attributed to any single lever. Decompose the bundle inside the phase cap, or report it as a bundle. `(supports 21-bloodmnist-cnn#C4)` `(supports 21-bloodmnist-cnn#C14)`
- **On a MedMNIST official split, run the near-duplicate audit first and state the split unit.** The seeding study dropped 11 cross-split near-duplicates from the evaluation partitions before training and stated that the split is image-level rather than patient-grouped. Its scores answer "new cells from the same smears and lab", never "a new patient". `(supports 21-bloodmnist-cnn#C15)`
- **Report a sealed comparison against a published number in binomial standard errors at the test size.** The sealed configuration scored accuracy 0.9527 on 3413 test images, 0.0053 below the MedMNIST v2 ResNet-18 (28) reference of 0.958. That gap is about 1.5 binomial standard errors of 0.0034, so the two are indistinguishable at this test size, whatever the arithmetic verdict of a "below the reference" rule. Call a gap under two standard errors indistinguishable. `(supports 21-bloodmnist-cnn#C5)` `(supports 21-bloodmnist-cnn#C16)`
- **Pin per-class recall, because the headline hides uneven effects.** From the anchor to the final configuration, basophil recall rose from 0.2562 to 0.9201. Monocyte recall fell from 0.8345 to 0.7949 at the last step, and monocytes stayed the weakest class on the sealed test at 0.8582. The study judged that drop to be within per-class noise. A deployment that cares about one class should look at that class first. `(supports 21-bloodmnist-cnn#C9)`
- **Better training did not cost calibration here.** Expected calibration error fell from 0.0828 at the anchor to 0.0125 at the final configuration (0.0086 on the sealed test), and log-loss fell with it. Measure calibration; do not assume a trade-off. `(supports 21-bloodmnist-cnn#C10)`
- **Run the label-shuffled harness control during the anchor phase.** The seeding study did not, so its harness check rests on analytic chance levels, the notary's near-constant-prediction rejection and a replication. The referee accepted this as a disclosed gap, not as a control. `(supports 21-bloodmnist-cnn#C17)`

## Open questions this domain inherits

- Which lever carried the bundle's +0.0501? The study's §⑦ item 1 is three registered cells at three seeds each, removing augmentation, the extra steps and the third block in turn. That is also the only way to resolve flip/rotation augmentation, which alone was unresolved at 1500 steps.
- How wide is the item-noise bar at the incumbent's level? The bar was measured on the anchor's confusion tables near macro-F1 0.72, and it is probably wider than the item noise near 0.95. That has not been measured.
- How well does a model trained on an image-level split transfer to a new patient, smear or site? An image-level split cannot answer this.
